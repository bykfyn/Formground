Designer

Olga Engel

Visit site →

Olga Engel is a product and interior designer who makes limited-edition furniture and lighting. Her lighting includes the Zephyr ceiling lights and mirrors, built from hundreds of biscuit porcelain pendants, and the Moonpapier lights made from porcelain petals on stainless steel.

Her seating runs from the upholstered Pompon, Boxes and Who Are You armchairs to the oak Babushka armchairs and the Lamb bench. Each work is shown as a limited edition with an enquiry option on her site. On Formground her work is shown through 48 works, each linked to her own website.
