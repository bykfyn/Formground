Designer

Thom Fougere

Visit site →

Thom Fougere is a Canadian designer working in Montréal across furniture, lighting, objects, interiors and architecture. He began his career running the design department of an international furniture manufacturer, was Creative Director of the Winnipeg design house EQ3 from 2010 to 2019, and set up his own studio in Montréal in 2021.

His pieces are made with craftspeople and partners, among them Mjolk in Toronto, EQ3, ThreeH and Ourse: tambour-fronted oak and walnut cabinets, saddle-leather and steel chairs, and the Tyndall series in stone. On Formground his work is shown through 33 works, each linked to his own website.
