Studio Niko Koronis is a multidisciplinary design studio in Milan, Italy, working on collectible furniture and larger interior environments. Its practice rests on material experimentation: tables, consoles and benches are carved from single blocks of marble and travertine, cast in resin, made in hand-painted glass, or built in lacquered wood and brass, with a few lamps and an armchair in the mix.

Works are produced as numbered or limited editions, and select works are shown through Carpenters Workshop Gallery, Studiotwentyseven and The Collectional, alongside commissions handled by the studio. On Formground the studio is shown through 84 works, each linked to its own website.
