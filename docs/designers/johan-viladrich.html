Designer

Johan Viladrich

France

Montpellier

Visit site →

Johan Viladrich is a French designer, born in Paris in 1991 and based in Montpellier, who works on furniture and spatial structures. His pieces are built from standard industrial profiles, plates and tubes in aluminium, bronze, copper and steel, joined with visible bolts or rubber bands and finished by brushing and wax, so that little trace of the maker's hand remains.

Benches, consoles, shelves and tables are made in numbered editions, some developed with Lauriane Heim as Heim+Viladrich, and the Paris gallery Kolkhoze offers several of them on request. On Formground his work is shown through 34 works, each linked to his own website.
