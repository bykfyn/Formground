Designer

Rooms

Georgia

Tbilisi · Design studio · Founded 2007

Visit site →

Rooms is a design studio in Tbilisi, Georgia, founded in 2007 by Nata Janberidze and Keti Toloraia. It started in interiors, with hotels, restaurants and homes in Georgia and abroad, and has since built up its own body of furniture, lighting, rugs and objects in reclaimed oak, terrazzo, stone and brass, often made with Georgian artisans.

The work draws on Georgian cultural memory, from Soviet bus-stop benches to dowry blankets, and has been shown at galleries and fairs in Paris, London, Milan and New York. Some pieces are made in numbered editions through Kolkhoze in Paris, and a ceramics series was made for Bitossi. On Formground the studio is shown through 80 works, each linked to its own website.
