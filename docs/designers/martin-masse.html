Designer

Martin Massé

France

Paris · Founded 2017

Visit site →

Martin Massé is an architect, interior designer and furniture designer based in Paris. He founded his studio in 2017 after working with Joseph Dirand Architecture, Tristan Auer and Wilmotte & Associés, and treats furniture as part of the same language as architecture, light and material.

His collections include Argos in blue-stained oak, Ippico in ziricote and travertine, Maen in lacquer or marble, and the upholstered Orso. They are made by hand in France as limited, numbered and signed editions and shown through international galleries. On Formground his work is shown through 100 works, each linked to his own website.
