Designer

Konstantin Grcic

Visit site →

Konstantin Grcic (born 1965) trained as a cabinet maker at the John Makepeace School for Craftsmen in Wood before studying design at the Royal College of Art in London. He set up his own practice in 1991, and Konstantin Grcic Design is now based in Berlin, working on furniture, products and lighting as well as exhibition design and collaborations in architecture and fashion.

His producers include Magis, Vitra, Flos and Cassina. On Formground he is credited on 39 pieces shown through Galerie Kreo, among them tables, benches, lounge chairs and lamps.
