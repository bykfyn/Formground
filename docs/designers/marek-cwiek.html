Marek Cwiek is a Polish-born sculptor and designer, born in Cieszyn in 1987, who lives and works in Málaga, Spain. He works mainly in stoneware, with finishes and glazes he develops himself, and has recently added metal: the Divergent Motions coffee table and the Strain & Release lamp combine stoneware with steel.

His vertical and horizontal sculptures draw on ancient temple foundations and on brutalist and postmodern architecture. Each sculpture is one of a kind and shown through Studiotwentyseven in New York. On Formground his work is shown through 16 works, each linked to his own website.
