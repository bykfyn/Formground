Abel Cárcamo

France

Paris

Visit site →

Abel Cárcamo is a Chilean-born designer and artist working from a studio and workshop near Paris. He models his pieces by hand in plaster and has them cast in bronze or aluminium, alongside work in marble, wood and ceramic, in collections such as Mutare, Arco, Morphe and Penzai.

Some series, among them Vanko, Cava and Archaïque, are made with his brother, the architect David Cárcamo, and much of the work is produced with craftspeople in Chile and France. Pieces are limited editions, available on request or through galleries including Studiotwentyseven in New York. On Formground his work is shown through 44 works, each linked to his own website.
