Krystian Kowalski

Visit site →

Krystian Kowalski is a Warsaw-based industrial designer, raised in a family of sculptors, who treats design as an analytical process and explores the language of objects through form, materiality and technology. He studied at the Royal College of Art in London (MA 2010) and the Academy of Fine Arts in Warsaw.

He set up his own practice in 2013 after working at studios in Warsaw and Milan, co-founded Kompott Studio, and was Creative Director at Tylko from 2016 to 2021. Today he designs for furniture and product brands. On Formground he is shown through 24 of his projects, each linked to its own page on his site.
