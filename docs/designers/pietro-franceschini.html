Designer

Pietro Franceschini

Visit site →

Pietro Franceschini is an Italian architect and designer, born in Florence in 1988, who runs PF Studio in Milan, founded in 2020. The studio makes collectible furniture and objects: the Arch benches, consoles and stools in brass, aluminium, steel, plaster and nabuk, Bellissimo tables in travertine, and the Slice Me Up coffee tables.

Upholstered seating includes the Bling Bling, Tomyris and Les Incroyables series. The work is shown through galleries such as Galerie Philia and Movimento, and by enquiry to the studio. On Formground his work is shown through 69 works, each linked to his own website.
