Designer

Frédéric Saulou

Rennes, France · Founded 2013

Visit site →

Frédéric Saulou is a French designer born in 1989 who lives and works in Rennes, Brittany. He founded Frédéric Saulou Design Studio in 2013 and treats each project as a small sculpture between simplicity and technique. His best-known pieces are tables, consoles, mirrors and lamps in slate and ornamental stone, many from the Domestiquer project, which began in 2015 after a residency in his native Anjou and draws on local mineral materials.

Work is made in limited editions and with partners such as Ligne Roset, Drugeot Manufacture and Brossier Saderne. On Formground his work is shown through 89 works, each linked to his own website.
