New York · Gallery and online shop · Founded 2018

Visit site →

Studiotwentyseven is an art and design gallery for contemporary, iconic, limited-edition and collectible design, started in Miami in 2018 by Nacho Polo, who is also its creative director, with Robert Onuska as director. It represents 68 designers and artists, from emerging names to historical figures such as Gio Ponti, Joe Colombo and Serge Mouille.

In 2024 the gallery opened its flagship in the Textile Building in Tribeca, New York, a 7,000 square foot space, and it also shows in Miami and London. The furniture, lighting, rugs and objects are made to order. On Formground its designers link to their own pages where we carry them, and to their pages at the gallery where we do not.
