Warm minimal living room

Warm minimal is a living room with little in it and nothing cold: oak and walnut in their natural tone, wool and bouclé in oat and sand, matte ceramics, and lamps that give a low, even light. Edges are rounded and colour stays quiet, so the pieces sit together without matching. Eight makers, one room.
