Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026.

Formground doesn't have accounts, logins, or profiles — there's very little of yours to collect in the first place. This page lays out plainly what little we do collect, why, and who else sees it.

What we collect

Searches and clicks. When you search, we log the search text itself, and if you click through to a maker's, architect's, or craftsperson's own site, we log which one and what query led there. This is stored with a timestamp, not with anything that identifies you personally — no name, no email, no account, no cookie tying it back to you specifically. It's used in aggregate, to understand what people are generally looking for and which makers are being found.

Basic site analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless by design — it doesn't set a tracking cookie or build a profile of you across visits. It gives us aggregate numbers (roughly how many people visited, what pages, what device/browser type) without identifying individual visitors.

The contact form. If you use our contact form, we collect whatever you enter — your name, email, and message, plus optional fields like your brand, website, or social profile if you fill them in. This is submitted through Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service, which delivers it to our inbox. We use it only to reply to you.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, no passwords, no login of any kind.
  • No tracking cookies, and no cross-site tracking.
  • No advertising pixels or third-party ad networks — Formground doesn't run ads.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone for marketing purposes.

Who else sees it

A few third-party services process data on our behalf, each for one specific job: Cloudflare (site hosting and the cookieless analytics above), Google Cloud / BigQuery (where anonymous search and click events are stored), and Web3Forms (processes contact form submissions and delivers them to us). None of them are authorized to use this data for their own purposes beyond providing that service to us.

How long we keep it

Search and click data is kept in aggregate for as long as it's useful for understanding overall site activity — it isn't tied to you individually, so there's no personal record to delete on that side. Contact form messages are kept as long as needed to handle your request, and are deleted on request.

Your rights

If you've contacted us and want to know what we have on file, or want it deleted, get in touch and we'll sort it out directly — there's no dashboard or self-service tool for this, but it's a simple email away.

Children

Formground isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this page changes in any meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top. Since Formground doesn't collect emails to notify you with, checking back here is the way to stay current.
