# Formground creator page text: template

Purpose: short, accurate, consistent text on each Creator page (Maker, Designer, Architect). Written to help a visitor decide whether to click through, and to be useful to AI search and agents.

## Length and shape
- 60-120 words in 2-3 short paragraphs, plus a facts block.
- Plain, factual, impartial tone. Same voice across all pages, in Formground's own voice: describe the creator directly, based on what their site and named sources say, as a fair reflection of their profile. Do not write "its own site says" or "it describes itself".

## What to include
1. **Who they are:** name, type (maker, designer, architect), base country and city.
2. **What they make or do:** the product types, materials and techniques, in concrete words (for example "pendant lamps in blown glass and brass").
3. **What distinguishes the work:** one specific, checkable detail from their own site (a process, a material, a series, a founding year, a collaboration).
4. **How they work:** made to order, limited editions, one-offs, open to commissions (only if they say so themselves).
5. **Where to find more:** link to their own site. Never Instagram as the main destination.

## Facts block (structured, consistent order)
Country | City | Type | Products | Work (architects) | Founded (if stated) | Made to order (only if stated) | Website

Leave a row out when it is unknown. No Materials row and no "independent or established" row.

Leave out what we do not know instead of guessing.

## What to leave out
- Superlatives and unsupported claims ("the best", "world-famous", "award-winning" unless the award is named and checkable).
- Anything not on their own site or a named source. No invented biography, quotes or prices.
- Comparisons that rank one creator above another.
- Personal details beyond what they publish professionally.
- Text copied from their site. Paraphrase, and keep any quote short and attributed.

## Quality checks before publishing
- Every factual statement can be found on their own site or a named source.
- The page adds something a visitor can't get from the headline alone. If it can't reach that bar, leave the page thin or hidden (the rule against thin pages).
- The "independent" label is only used if you have vouched for it directly.
- Remove-on-request is honoured: a creator who asks gets the text changed or removed.

## Example (illustrative only, not a real creator)
"Atelier Example is a lighting studio in Gothenburg, Sweden, working with blown glass and brass. Its pendant series is made to order in small batches in the studio. The studio was founded in 2015 and works with architects on larger installations.

Pieces are made to order and lead times are stated on their site."
