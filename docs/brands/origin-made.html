Origin Made is a design brand in Porto, Portugal, founded by Gabriel Tan and Cherie Er. It sells furniture, lighting, textiles and collectible objects, including rugs, vases and an Experimental Editions line.

Pieces are designed by Tan and invited designers and made by makers the brand describes as small family-run workshops, solo artists and skilled craftspeople. Its stated mission is to preserve vanishing craft cultures through design, and its showroom is on Rua do Almada. On Formground it is shown through 81 pieces, led by vases, sculptures and rugs, each linked to its own page.
