Visit site →

Multiforme is a Murano glass lighting maker in Trebaseleghe, near Padua in Italy, founded in 2009. It makes chandeliers, table lamps, ceiling fixtures, floor lamps and wall lights, with its glassworks and offices in the Veneto lighting district.

Besides the standard range, it designs and builds custom and oversized chandeliers with architects and designers, makes prototypes and mock-ups, and restores historic chandeliers. On Formground it is shown through 472 pieces, each linked to its own page.
