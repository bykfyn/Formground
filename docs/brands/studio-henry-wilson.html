Studio Henry Wilson is a design studio founded in inner Sydney, Australia, in 2012 by Henry Wilson, who studied woodwork at the Australian National University and holds a master's degree from Design Academy Eindhoven.

The studio designs lighting, furniture, objects and hardware in metal, stone and glass, with elements cast in bronze and aluminium by a local foundry and stonework from European stonemasons. Pieces are made by hand, and an idea may move between forms: a lamp can become a hook, bookend or chair. On Formground it is shown through 44 pieces of its own design, each linked to its own page.
