Alessi

FurnitureLightingCeramicsObjectsItaly

Founded 1921

Visit site →

Alessi is an Italian maker of design objects, active since 1921 and calling itself the Dream Factory. For over a century it has made objects meant to bring poetry and emotion into everyday life, working with more than 900 designers, architects and artists.

Its catalogue holds more than 1,400 creations, and many pieces have become icons housed in major art and design museums. On Formground it is shown through 608 pieces, each linked to its own page.
