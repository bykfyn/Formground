Grupa is a design studio and manufacturer in Zagreb, Croatia, founded in 2006 by Filip Despot, Ivana Pavić and Tihana Taraba. In 2012 it began making its own lighting line, and its collections include Onda, Okolo, Arigato, Baluna and Ili Ili.

All Grupa products are designed by the studio and manufactured and assembled by hand in Croatia, mostly at a lighting factory that dates from 1960. On Formground it is shown through 44 pieces, each linked to its own page.
