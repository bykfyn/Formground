Arno Declercq is a furniture and object studio in Belgium, with a showroom in Antwerp and ateliers in Zaventem. The studio works in wood burnt by hand with the Japanese shou sugi ban technique, charring oak, ash, walnut and Iroko until the surface turns deep black, and pairs it with bronze for tableware and objects.

The range covers sofas, armchairs, benches, stools, tables, consoles and storage, along with trays, bowls, vases and candle holders, arranged in collections such as Fundament, Umbo and Zoumey. The studio also takes bespoke and made-to-measure commissions for residential and hospitality projects. On Formground it is shown through 75 pieces, each linked to its own page.
