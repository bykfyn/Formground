Yuxuan Huang is a furniture and lighting designer based between Brooklyn, New York, and China. She works with reclaimed wood, bamboo, stone and paper, and her recent lamps pair hand-painted paper and shellac shades on bamboo frames with salvaged wood or stone bases.

Her Lost Stories collection takes apart antique dressers and rebuilds them as cabinets, a coffee table and a chair, keeping the marks of use. All works are available to commission in custom sizes, and a small shop lists the Seda sconce and Dream Catcher on Rock. On Formground it is shown through 18 pieces, each linked to its own page.
