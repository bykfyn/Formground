Armadillo

ObjectsAustralia
Visit site →

Armadillo is a rug house with Australian roots, making hand-knotted and flat-woven rugs in wool, jute, linen and silk. The range is organised by technique and by collection, including Venezia, GAIA, Meridian and Linea, in sizes from runners to large room formats, with custom colours crafted to order.

The company has been a Certified B Corporation since 2021 and shows its rugs in ten showrooms across North America, Europe and Australasia. On Formground it is shown through 70 rug designs, each linked to its own page on its US store.
