Unico Milano

Visit site →

Unico Milano is an online furnishing brand, Milanese in name and outlook but sourcing its products from suppliers around the world rather than making them in Milan or Italy. The range covers sideboards, seats, tables, coffee tables, lights and tableware, chosen for a minimalist, functional look.

The brand sells through its own online shop, with a separate page for trade customers and a "shop the look" section that groups pieces together. On Formground it is shown through 220 pieces, each linked to its own page.
