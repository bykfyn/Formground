Furn Object is a Ukrainian furniture and decor brand founded in Odesa in 2022 by the designers Anna and Sergii Baierzdorf. Its collectible pieces in metal, glass and upholstery include the Prowling sofa, lounge chair, bench and ottoman, the Bryla cabinets, the Liquid Table and Liquid Rack in steel or brass, the Valun seating and the Lucky Akebia glass lamps.

Everything is handcrafted by Ukrainian artisans, and the Liquid pieces use an old hand-chasing technique that gives the metal a textured surface. Made-to-order lead times are stated as 6 to 12 weeks, customisation is offered, and prices are given on request. On Formground it is shown through 20 pieces, each linked to its own page.
