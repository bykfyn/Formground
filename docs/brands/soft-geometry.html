Soft Geometry is a design studio in Los Angeles, founded in 2019 by Utharaa L Zacharias and Palaash Chaudhary, working across lighting, objects and furniture. The practice builds softness into its forms through slow, hand-built processes and moves to new materials and techniques each year.

The collection includes the Elio lamps, Long Haired Sconces, the resin Molecule sculptures and coffee table, the Donut coffee table, a bent steel Vessel vase and the SW chairs and tables. Molecule is made to order. On Formground the studio is shown through 11 pieces, each linked to its own page.
