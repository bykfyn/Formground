Rye is a Danish bedroom furniture brand with its own production facilities in the Danish Lake Highlands. Its bed frames come in oak finishes such as oiled, smoked and soaped oak, and the range also includes a Solido side table.

Production follows a made-to-order model, which the brand ties to working in small volumes and with little waste. One bed frame, Veng, was made in collaboration with Re-wool. On Formground it is shown through 41 pieces, each linked to its own page.
