SOWDEN is the lighting and objects brand of George Sowden, who was born in Leeds, moved to Milan in 1970, opened his own studio there in 1979 and was a founding member of the Memphis Group in 1981. The brand dates from 2010 and is designed in Milan.

Its lamps are made of silicone and come in portable, table, ceiling and floor versions, the PL, TL, CL and FL series. The range also includes Pepper Salt and the Thing tables made with raawii. On Formground it is shown through 27 pieces, each linked to its own page.
