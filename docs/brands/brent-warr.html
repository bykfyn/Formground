Brent Warr is a furniture and lighting studio in Bainbridge, Georgia, led by designer Brent Warr, who studied at Auburn University and worked for Meyer Davis Studio and Yabu Pushelberg in New York before returning to Georgia to start his own studio. The pieces are sculptural: chairs, benches, tables, mirrors, chandeliers, pendants and lamps, together with vases and candle holders, built in wood, steel mesh and plaster and fibreglass composites.

Pieces are made to order, with the lead time and size stated on each piece's page. On Formground the studio is shown through 51 pieces, each linked to its own page.
