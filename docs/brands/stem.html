Stem is a design studio in Noida, India, led by Aman Bhayana and Sugandhi Mehrotra. It works across furniture, lighting and objects, including table, floor and wall lamps, desk objects, candle holders and a coat rack.

The studio blends Indian craftsmanship with contemporary design, with skilled artisans involved by hand in every step of making. Its range includes the Pivot Table Lamp, Aria Linear Light and Ito Floor Lamp. On Formground it is shown through 27 pieces, each linked to its own page.
