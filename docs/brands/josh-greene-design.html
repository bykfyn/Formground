Josh Greene Design is an interior design studio in New York that also makes its own product lines. Its oak-framed medicine cabinets, launched in 2021, come in seven designs (Caracalla, Kallis, Redondo, Sento, Sutro, Tamina and Vals), each available as a cabinet or as a plain mirror, in stained, whitewashed and painted finishes.

The studio also offers hand-knotted wool rugs in custom sizes, alongside wallpaper, fabric and a furniture collection made with Lawson-Fenning. On Formground it is shown through 13 pieces, each linked to its own page.
