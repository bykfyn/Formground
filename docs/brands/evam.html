Evam

FurnitureLightingUnited States

New York · Made to order

Visit site →

Evam is a New York design studio founded by furniture designer Caroline Kable. Its collection covers stools, chairs, benches, tables and a sofa, along with wall lights and pendants, in materials such as hand-carved reclaimed teak, embossed copper and stone.

The pieces are made to order with manufacturing partners in India and elsewhere, starting with workshops in Udaipur, and each carries a stated lead time. On Formground it is shown through 24 pieces, each linked to its own page.
