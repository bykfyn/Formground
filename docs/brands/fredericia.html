Fredericia is a Danish furniture company founded in 1911 in the city of Fredericia and still owned by the Graversen family. It began as Fredericia Stolefabrik, a chair factory, and from 1955 worked with Børge Mogensen, whose Spanish Chair and J39 are in the range. It acquired Erik Jørgensen in 2020.

Selected designs are produced in Fredericia and Svendborg, and the company is a certified B Corp. Spine, Delphi and Pato are among the pieces shown here. On Formground it is shown through 150 pieces, each linked to its own page.
