Devin Wilde is an artist and ceramicist based in Brooklyn, New York, who founded his design studio in Red Hook in 2023. Trained in architecture at Stanford, he spent several years at artist residencies in ceramics and wood, including Anderson Ranch Arts Center and Penland School of Craft.

The studio makes vessels, ceramic tables and lighting, including the Staccato Series developed with Astraeus Clarke. Each piece is made to order, by hand, in the Red Hook studio. On Formground it is shown through 19 pieces, each linked to its own page.
