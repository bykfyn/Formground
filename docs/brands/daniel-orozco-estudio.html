Daniel Orozco Estudio is an interior design and decor studio with its main showroom and workshop in Tulum, Mexico, and a second showroom in Mexico City. It makes furniture, lighting and sculptural objects, and takes on hotel, private interior and custom projects.

The workshop turns and shapes pieces by hand in locally sourced woods from Quintana Roo, such as cedar, jabín and parota. The studio follows a zero-waste approach, making smaller pieces from leftovers of larger projects, and works with local artisans. On Formground it is shown through 247 pieces, mostly sculptures and lamps, each linked to its own page.
