Monsieur Cailloux

CeramicsFrance
Visit site →

Monsieur Cailloux is a ceramic artist based in Paris, France, who began this work in 2020. The artist makes sculptural earthenware figures called Cailloux, presented as inhabitants of an imaginary planet named MRCX, each shown with its own number and measurements.

Every piece is hand-modelled and glazed, and is described as unique. The work has been shown in solo and group exhibitions, including at YTF Gallery in Taipei and Kaplan Projects in Mallorca. On Formground it is shown through 92 pieces, each linked to its own page.
