Dina No is an artist in Portland, Oregon, who also makes functional ceramics. Her work draws on traditional Korean craft and architecture, along with memes and doodles, and returns to the tension between what is visible and what is not, and between form and function.

Her shop offers wheel-thrown stoneware mugs, bowls, plates and small kitchen pieces such as a jar, a ramekin and a pour-over, with hand-made handles and glazes that vary from piece to piece. On Formground it is shown through 15 pieces, each linked to its own page.
