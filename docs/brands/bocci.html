Bocci is a lighting maker headquartered in Vancouver, Canada, founded in 2005 under the creative direction of Omer Arbel. It makes glass and metal lighting and installations, and numbers each project in the order it was conceived, so its lamps are grouped in series such as 14, 28 and 141.

Every object is developed, engineered and fabricated in-house at the Vancouver factory, and each piece is handmade and therefore unique. Bocci also offers an online configurator for building an installation to suit a space. On Formground it is shown through 48 pieces, each linked to its own page.
