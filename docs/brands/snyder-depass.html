Snyder DePass is a furniture and art studio in Los Angeles, founded by Jonathan Snyder and Alejandro DePass. The studio works in solid wood and draws on traditional joinery, the natural world and 20th-century architecture.

It makes both cohesive collections and one-off pieces, from dining tables and coffee tables to stools, side tables and a lamp, and has shown work in group exhibitions including Lamp Show and Small Object Show at AUTOBODY in 2025. Commissions are offered through the site. On Formground it is shown through 17 pieces, each linked to its own page.
