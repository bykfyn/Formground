SKLO is a lighting and design brand founded in 2009 in the wine country of Northern California, with its glass made in the Czech Republic. It was founded by Pavel Hanousek with Karen, a craft artist and jeweller, and Paul, a designer, combining a modern California outlook with Czech glassblowing.

The pieces are handmade and hand-blown, in a tradition the site traces to the 13th century, and the brand embraces the irregularity of glass. It makes pendants, chandeliers, wall lamps, vases, sculptures and tabletop pieces. On Formground it is shown through 92 pieces, each linked to its own page.
