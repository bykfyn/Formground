Fern

FurnitureLightingObjectsUnited States

Hudson, New York · Founded 2009 · Made to order

Visit site →

Fern is a furniture and lighting studio in Hudson, New York, founded in 2009 by Jason Roskey in Brooklyn and now working from a studio and workshop in the Hudson River Valley. Roskey designs every piece, and the studio produces them in-house, mostly in American hardwoods such as walnut, maple, cherry and white oak, alongside leather, bronze and brass.

The range covers dining and low tables, case pieces, seating, beds, lamps and leather objects. Pieces are generally custom in size and material and made to order. On Formground it is shown through 77 pieces, each linked to its own page.
