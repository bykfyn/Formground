SEM is a Milan furniture maker, born as a spin-off of the Milanese brand Spotti and drawing on 70 years of craft knowledge and relationships with local workshops. It makes collectible furniture in unlimited, customisable series, answering a demand for personalised, finely made design that high-volume producers cannot offer.

Each collection is made with a different designer: Hannes Peer (Core, Paesaggio), Studio Paolo Ferrari (Arche), Motta Architecture (Néolithique), Giacomo Moor (Pivot), Zaven (In Sync), Marcante-Testa (Futuraforma) and Valentina Cameranesi Sgroi (Simultanea). On Formground it is shown through 36 pieces, each linked to its collection page.
