Eilersen is a furniture maker based in Skamby, Denmark, with a history that goes back to 1895. It makes sofas, chairs, tables, shelves and accessories, and offers fabrics and leather for its upholstery. Its sofas are built on a solid pinewood frame, with timber oven-dried to a moisture content of 8-10 percent and traceable to FSC-certified sources.

The range is split into the main Eilersen collection and separate Contract, Outdoor and Viable lines. Eilersen has showrooms in Skamby and Copenhagen and a dealer section for trade customers. On Formground it is shown through 67 pieces, each linked to its own page.
