Imperfettolab is a family-based design laboratory and gallery in Longiano, in Italy's Emilia-Romagna region. It makes furniture, lighting, mirrors, shelving and objects, as well as limited-edition pieces, with a sculptural character. The work is built in fibreglass and sanded by hand, so the surface keeps the marks of the making.

Next to the workshop sits Imperfettoart, a gallery space. On Formground the lab is shown through 89 pieces, from seating and tables to lamps, mirrors and objects, each linked to its own page.
