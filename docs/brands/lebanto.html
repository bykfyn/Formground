Lebanto

FurnitureObjectsItaly

Cantù, Como · Made to order

Visit site →

Lebanto is a contemporary furniture brand in Cantù, between Milan and Lake Como, Italy. Its tables and mirrors are built around natural stone, including Calacatta Viola, Roman travertine and Emperador Dark, combined with wood and hand-finished glass.

Production starts only when an order is placed, and the pieces are made in Italy in small wood and furniture workshops in the surrounding district. The brand works with architects, interior designers and showrooms in Europe and the United States. On Formground it is shown through 26 pieces, each linked to its own page.
