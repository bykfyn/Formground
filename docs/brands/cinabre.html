Cinabre is a gallery and foundry in Paris, founded by Jacques and Christine Rouvière, making art furniture and lighting inspired by nature. Its collections draw on bronze casting, where each piece starts from a sculpted mold that captures texture and organic form.

The range covers wall lights, table and floor lamps, chandeliers, mirrors, coffee tables, consoles, dining tables and cabinets, in bronze combined with selenite, marble, slate, brass and oak. Most pieces carry the name of Christine D. Rouvière as artist. Prices are given on enquiry. On Formground it is shown through 132 pieces, each linked to its own page.
