Ross Gardam is a furniture and lighting studio in Brunswick, Melbourne, Australia, run by a team of designers, engineers and makers who work together from idea to finished piece. All products are designed and produced in Melbourne.

The lighting includes Aeris, Ceto, Liminal and Relic, and the furniture includes the Dwell, Hearth and Adapt lounge systems. Lights are hand-assembled in the studio from locally sourced glass, timber, ceramic and machined metal. On Formground it is shown through 66 pieces, each linked to its own page.
