Sophie Lou Jacobsen is a design studio in New York, led by the French-American designer of the same name and founded in 2019. It works across homewares, lighting and collectibles, with glass drinkware and serveware a large part of the range.

The studio's work is organised in collections such as Disco, Aperitivo, Continental and Bouquet, alongside sconces, pendants and table lamps. Some collectibles are limited editions sold by inquiry. On Formground it is shown through 110 pieces, each linked to its own page.
