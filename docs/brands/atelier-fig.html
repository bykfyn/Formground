Atelier Fig is the Arnhem studio of Ruben Hoogvliet and Gijs Wouters, who met while studying at the ArtEZ art academy. In the porcelain Gravity collection, absorbent structures are dipped in liquid clay and left to drip dry, so gravity shapes every vase, bowl and candle holder differently.

The Fringe tableware and clocks grew out of cutting clay with a wire, and the Frame Cabinet is built from beech and aluminium. Much of the work is made to order, some in limited editions. On Formground the studio is shown through 23 pieces, each linked to the studio's website.
