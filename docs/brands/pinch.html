Pinch is a London furniture and lighting company founded in 2004 by Russell Pinch and Oona Bannon. Its look is quiet and elegant, with a focus on craft: pieces are made by skilled craftspeople in the UK and Europe, and designed to endure and be inherited.

It holds stock of many pieces, can finish some in bespoke colours, and takes custom orders. It sells from its showroom in London's Pimlico Road district and ships worldwide. On Formground it is shown through 161 pieces.
