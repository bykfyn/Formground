Ariela Nomi Kuh is a ceramicist in Maine who works under the name ANK Studios, with a tableware line called ANK Ceramics begun in 2013. Her own-name work is one-of-a-kind, hand-built vessels, platters, bowls, vases and sculptural pieces, fired in an oxidation kiln.

The ANK line is slip-cast mugs and vases in hand-mixed glazes, with seasonal menorahs and pitchers added through the year. Most pieces are single originals, listed one by one. On Formground it is shown through 31 pieces, each linked to its own page.
