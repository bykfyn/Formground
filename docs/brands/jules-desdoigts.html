Jules Desdoigts is a Paris studio making furniture and lighting in bronze. The designer trained at Penninghen and ECAL, worked for several years with Christian Liaigre, and set up his own studio in 2021.

The Sauvage collection is sculpted by hand in the atelier and cast in bronze, with black-patinated finishes. It includes candleholders, wall lights, a floor lamp, a desk, a side table and a coffee table, with paper or glass elements on some pieces. Prices are given on request. On Formground it is shown through 10 pieces, each linked to its own page.
