Slush Club is a ceramics studio in Tacoma, Washington, where Shannon Eakins designs and hand-builds every piece. Set up in 2022 with Marc Dombrosky, the studio adapts the nerikomi technique of stacking several clay bodies into patterns, colouring locally sourced clays with stains added directly to the clay.

The work is food-safe, dishwasher-friendly dinnerware: mugs, cups, bowls, plates and platters, each numbered and made in limited runs. Most of it goes through stockists, with a smaller changing selection in the studio's own shop. On Formground it is shown through 27 pieces from that shop, each linked to its own page.
