Atelier Areti is a design and manufacturing studio for lighting and furniture, founded in 2008 by Gwendolyn and Guillane Kerschbaumer, with bases in the UK and Germany. Its workshop is in the Sauerland region of Germany and works mainly with metal, glass, stone and wood. Each light in the Disc and Sphere Glass series has a handmade glass disc with its own pattern.

The studio offers customisations and bespoke pieces, including single pieces and small series, with designers, architects, lighting technicians and craftspeople following each project from concept to delivery. On Formground it is shown through 159 pieces, each linked to its own page.
