nanimarquina is a rug house founded in 1987 by Nani Marquina, which opened its first showroom in Barcelona in 2010. Its rugs are hand-knotted, hand-loomed or hand-tufted in India, Pakistan and Nepal, in wool, cotton, jute, nettle and recycled PET.

Designs come from Nani Marquina and from collaborators including Ronan and Erwan Bouroullec, Jaime Hayon, Eduardo Chillida and Lucia Eames. Chillida, Hayon x Nani, Lucia Eames, Mélange and Flora are among those shown here. On Formground it is shown through 151 pieces, each linked to its own page.
