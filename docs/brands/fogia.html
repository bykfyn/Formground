Fogia is a Swedish furniture maker whose work since 1981 has been shaped by craftsmanship, material knowledge and close collaboration between designers and makers. Its own factory has wood workshops and studios for upholstery, cutting and sewing, so ideas can be tested and refined close to production. Its approach is function and friction: a piece needs both.

It has showrooms in Stockholm, Gothenburg and Oslo, with its flagship store in Stockholm. On Formground it is shown through 121 pieces, from sofas and lounge chairs to tables, shelves and lighting, each linked to its own page.
