Serax is a Belgian design brand based in Antwerp, founded in 1986 by brothers Serge and Axel Van Den Bossche and still run as a family business. It offers dinnerware, glassware, lighting, vases and pottery, home accessories and furniture.

Axel heads the company and curates each collection with the Antwerp team. Many pieces are developed with outside designers, among them Paola Navone, Vincent Van Duysen and Piet Boon, and are produced by craftspeople around the world in materials such as stone, wood, metal, glass and clay.

On Formground it is shown through 120 pieces, each linked to its own page.
