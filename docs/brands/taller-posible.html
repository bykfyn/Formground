Taller Posible

FurnitureLightingObjectsArgentina

Buenos Aires

Visit site →

Taller Posible is a furniture workshop in Buenos Aires, Argentina, with a shop in Retiro and its workshop in Paternal. It makes benches, tables, stools, chairs and bookcases in wood, plus a few accessories.

Its Tronco line came out of workshop experiments with cutting and fitting solid wood cylinders, in guatambú and incienso with a semi-matte hydrolacquer finish. Leibal notes the visible joinery on the Banco Doble Tronco. Delivery on that bench is listed as 60 to 75 days. On Formground it is shown through 48 pieces, mostly benches, each linked to its own page.
