Mexico City · Made to order

Visit site →

Ayres is a design studio in Mexico City, led by the designer Karim Molina, making sculptural and functional pieces in marble, travertine, onyx and volcanic rock. The collections cover consoles, side tables, stools and dining tables, lamps and vases, vessels and totems.

Every piece is handmade with artisans across Mexico in small batches, and pieces in the collections are priced on request. The studio is shown by Studio Fenice. On Formground it is shown through 38 pieces, each linked to its own page.
