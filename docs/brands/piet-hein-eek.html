Piet Hein Eek is a furniture maker in Eindhoven, the Netherlands, known for furniture and objects in scrap wood. The designer graduated from Design Academy Eindhoven in 1990 with a scrap wood cabinet that became a signature piece, and products and components are made in-house in a former Philips ceramics workshop that also holds a showroom, gallery, hotel and restaurants.

The range covers furniture, lighting, accessories, kitchens and ceramics, along with custom-made pieces and commissioned interiors. On Formground it is shown through 848 pieces, each linked to its own page.
