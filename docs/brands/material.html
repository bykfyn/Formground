Material is an architecture firm and furniture workshop in Portland, Oregon, founded by architect and fabricator Tim Fouch. The furniture is made in solid wood, mainly walnut, white oak and blackened ash: slatted credenzas, foundation stools and side tables, coffee tables, a gallery bench, a low bookcase and a wardrobe.

Pieces are handmade to order in the Portland workshop, and custom sizes and configurations are offered. On Formground it is shown through 14 pieces, each linked to its own page.
