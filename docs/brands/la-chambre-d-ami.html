La Chambre d'Ami is a French home decoration brand based in Rennes, Brittany. The designer, Rémi, creates and produces the objects in his own workshop, taking each one from first prototype to finished product. The range includes lamps in the Igloo, Pivoine, Méduse and Gaz series, as well as vases, soliflores and soap holders.

The brand redesigns everyday decorative objects into simplified, modular forms, and everything is made in France. Products are also sold through partner shops. On Formground it is shown through 21 pieces, each linked to its own page.
