COSEINCORSO is a design studio founded by Marzia Cerio and Marwann Frikach, working between craft, curation and contemporary design. Its shop covers ceramic vases and bowls inspired by historic apothecary pots, steel and stainless-steel mirrors, benches, tables, shelves, a wall lamp and a candle tray.

Pieces are handmade with artisans, and several are limited editions with the edition size stated on the product page. The studio also curates exhibitions. On Formground it is shown through 20 pieces, each linked to its own page.
