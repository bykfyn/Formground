Arwenn is a furniture retailer in Berlin, Germany. It sells furniture, lighting and accessories, including chairs, lounge chairs, sofas, dining tables, desks, floor and table lamps, mirrors and vases. It runs a physical store in Berlin built around a gallery and warehouse concept.

Arwenn focuses on items that are available directly, so buyers can go from choosing to owning without a long wait. A separate business channel serves professionals, covering contract conditions, samples, customization and planning support. On Formground it is shown through 36 pieces, each linked to its own page.
