SINGCHAN DESIGN is a design studio in Guangzhou, China, founded by Sing Chan in 2020. It works across lighting, furniture and objects, looking to different civilisations and eras for references.

The range includes the Monument sofas and armchair, the Thewall and Thegate tables and cabinets, Epang tables carved in teak, and the Fragment lighting series in stainless steel and brass. Pieces are shown for enquiry and sold through showrooms and stockists across China. On Formground it is shown through 32 pieces, each linked to its own page.
