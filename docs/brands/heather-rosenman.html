Heather Rosenman is a ceramic artist based in Los Angeles. A New York native, she studied at The Cooper Union and The Basel School of Design in Switzerland. She makes vessels and sculptures with graphic surfaces and lava glazes she developed through experimentation.

Her work draws on ancient forms, semiology and modern art, simplified to a gestural essence. Pieces are made to order and are available as they come out of the kiln, and she takes commissions directly. The shop groups work into series such as Leto, Scribe and Lava. On Formground it is shown through 54 pieces, each linked to its own page.
