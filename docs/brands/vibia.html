Vibia is a lighting company born in Barcelona, founded there in 1987 by Joan Blanqué and Ernest Vidal. It sells in more than 105 countries, with subsidiaries in New Jersey and Singapore and commercial branches in London, Frankfurt and Dubai.

The range is organised by how a lamp is installed: pendants such as Asia, Bind and Circus, ceiling lamps such as Pro and Duo, and outdoor lamps such as Closer and Break. Vibia designs around thin construction, glue-free assembly so parts can be taken apart, low-voltage LEDs and interchangeable components. On Formground it is shown through 137 pieces, each linked to its own page.
