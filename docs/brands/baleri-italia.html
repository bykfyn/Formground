Baleri Italia is a design furniture company founded on 8 March 1984 by Enrico Baleri and Marilisa Decimo, with its headquarters in Cantù, Italy. It built its name on work with designers including Philippe Starck, Hannes Wettstein, Angelo Mangiarotti and Alessandro Mendini.

The Cartoons screen received the Compasso d'Oro in 1994 and is in the permanent collection of the Museum of Modern Art in New York. The collection shown here includes the Juliette stools and chairs, the Spunta sofa and armchair, the Matteo modular unit and the Amiko coat hanger. On Formground it is shown through 59 pieces, each linked to its own page.
