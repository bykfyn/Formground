Pani Jurek is a design studio combined with a ceramics workshop in Grójec, Poland, run by Magda Jurek and Michał Borecki. It works between design, craft and art, and colour, which comes from Magda Jurek's background in painting, runs through the objects.

The collections include the TRN pendant lights, wall lamp, mirrors and tables, the OP standing lamps, ELO mirrors and BARVA vases. Pieces are held in the National Museum in Krakow and the National Museum in Warsaw. On Formground it is shown through 41 pieces, each linked to its own page.
