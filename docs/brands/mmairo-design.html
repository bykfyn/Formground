MMairo is a marble design company in Carrara, Italy, founded in 2016. It designs and produces marble objects in Tuscany, hand finished by local artisans, and works with a roster of international designers including Ivan Colominas, Karen Chekerdjian, Matteo Cibic and Jacopo Simonetti.

The range covers tables and stools, lamps, vases, candle holders, bowls, trays and plates, in marbles from Carrara and further afield. Several designs come as families, such as Inside Out, Nova and Tivoli, with matching tables and tableware. On Formground it is shown through 59 pieces, each linked to its own page.
