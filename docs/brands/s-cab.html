S-CAB is a furniture maker in Coccaglio, in the province of Brescia, Italy. Founded in 1957, it has specialised in seating since the 1960s, and the range covers chairs, armchairs and barstools as well as sofas, sunbeds, tables, table bases and coffee tables, for homes and for commercial spaces.

Production is Made in Italy across three plants near Brescia, working in plastic, steel, wood and fabric. The Go Green range is made from post-consumer recycled plastic, and many models come from outside designers such as Meneghello Paolelli and Marcello Ziliani, who drew the Lisa chair. On Formground it is shown through 114 pieces, each linked to its own page.
