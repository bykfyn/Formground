Kalon is a furniture studio founded in 2007 by Michaele Simmering and Johannes Pauwen and based in Los Angeles, with a showroom in Atwater Village that opened in 2021. It makes American-made furniture and objects in timber from sustainably managed forests, working with small regional teams of craftspeople.

Made-to-order pieces typically ship in six to twelve weeks. Rugosa, Material Studies, Highland and Caravan are among the collections shown here. On Formground it is shown through 79 pieces, each linked to its own page.
