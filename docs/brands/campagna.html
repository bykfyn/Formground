Campagna is a furniture studio in Portland, Oregon, making solid-wood furniture with traditional joinery and no applied ornament. The collection covers tables, chairs, a credenza and a coat rack, each built on simple geometric shapes, among them the Cross Bar, Narrows and Johann tables.

Boards are selected and matched for colour and grain, and most pieces can be made in other sizes or woods. Orders are placed by email, with a trade discount for design professionals, and some designs are also sold through Roll & Hill and Design Within Reach. On Formground it is shown through 12 pieces, each linked to its own page.
