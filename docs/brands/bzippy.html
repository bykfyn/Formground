BZIPPY is a ceramics studio in Los Angeles, founded in 2008 by artist Bari Ziperstein. It makes ceramic furniture, planters, vases and other housewares in open editions, handbuilt from slab-based and extruded forms inspired by architecture and industrial design.

The studio's work is divided between production ceramics, a collectible design line and Ziperstein's own artworks. Since 2019 it has also made one-of-a-kind ceramic furniture and large planters sold only through The Future Perfect. On Formground it is shown through 28 pieces, mostly side tables, planters and vases, each linked to its own page.
