Galerie Kreo

FurnitureLightingCeramicsObjectsFrance

Paris · Gallery · Founded 1999

Visit site →

Galerie kreo is a design gallery in Paris, founded in 1999 by Clémence and Didier Krzentowski. It produces contemporary furniture and objects with designers such as Konstantin Grcic, Jasper Morrison and Hella Jongerius, and it also shows French and Italian lighting from the 1950s to the 1980s. A second gallery opened in London's Mayfair in 2014.

The contemporary pieces are made in limited series, created by the designers exclusively for the gallery. The Paris gallery has been at 31 rue Dauphine in Saint-Germain-des-Prés since 2008. On Formground it is shown through 1,005 pieces, each linked to its own page.
