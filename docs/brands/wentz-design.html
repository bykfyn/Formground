Wentz is a Brazilian furniture, lighting and objects brand founded by the designer Guilherme Wentz, with its design and development studio in Caxias do Sul and a gallery in São Paulo. The range covers armchairs, sofas, chairs, tables, beds, pendant, wall, floor and table lamps, vases and smaller objects.

The work starts from research into materials, processes and forms, and the brand names its series: Fungi lighting, the Tábua tables with a recycled-PET core, Dobra modular seating and the Objects for Silence collection, among others. Many pieces are customisable, and some are limited editions. On Formground it is shown through 97 pieces, each linked to its own page.
