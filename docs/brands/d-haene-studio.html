D-Haene Studio is a ceramic and design studio in Brooklyn, New York, created by artist Jane Yang-D'Haene. Born and raised in Korea, she draws on the forms of traditional Korean pottery, the moon jar in particular, and gives them new surfaces, glazes and textures in hand-built stoneware.

The work is organised in series: Moon, Untitled, Terrain and Dune vessels, hand-built bowls, side tables, the Jogakbo table in oak and glazed ceramic tiles, and the Mizutama ceramic table lamp. Pieces are made to order, with the lead time stated on the site. On Formground it is shown through 54 pieces, each linked to its own page.
