Mingardo is a metal furniture maker in Monselice, Padua, Italy. Daniele Mingardo launched the brand at Milan Design Week in 2013, from the blacksmith workshop his father Ilario opened in 1970. It makes small numbered editions in brass, copper, iron and steel, with designers including Simone Bonanni (Tartan), Elisa Honkanen (Shirudo), Chiara Andreatti (Satin Table), Marco Zito (Asse Zeta) and Omri Revesz (Fabbrica).

The workshop also builds custom-made furniture and metalwork for interiors, hotels and shops. On Formground it is shown through 40 pieces, each linked to its own page.
