Kassandra Thatcher is a ceramic lighting and sculpture studio working between Los Angeles and New York. Her practice spans ceramic, plaster and metal, and the table, floor and wall lamps are hand-finished stoneware with brass or steel hardware.

Pieces are made to order by hand, with a stated lead time of 6 to 8 weeks. The debut sconce series was made with her mother from a single rock collected on the coast of Montauk. On Formground it is shown through 28 pieces, linked to the studio's collection pages.
