Collection Particulière is a Paris design house, launched in 2014 by Jérôme Aumont, that publishes furniture, lighting and objects by invited designers. Much of the range is by Christophe Delcourt, alongside pieces by Dan Yeffet, Luca Erba, Yabu Pushelberg and others.

The pieces are drawn in oak and walnut, marble and travertine, bronze and ceramic, and each page names where it is made: mostly France, with some marble pieces made in Italy and Portugal. A few are numbered limited editions. On Formground it is shown through 92 pieces, each linked to its own page.
