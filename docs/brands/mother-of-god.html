Mother of God is a ceramics studio in Austin, Texas, run by the self-taught ceramicists Diana Welch and Amanda Rivera. It is an experimental practice, exploring what an object can become through collaboration, with a focus on limited-edition pieces that sit between sculpture and function.

The shop covers coil-built table lamps and pendants, one-of-a-kind Pedestal side tables in satin glazes, and the Muir tableware series of serving bowls and trays. The studio also teaches classes in lamp-making and tableware. On Formground it is shown through 14 pieces, each linked to its own page.
