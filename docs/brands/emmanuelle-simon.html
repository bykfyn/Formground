Emmanuelle Simon is a Franco-Israeli interior architect and designer who founded her studio in Paris in 2017. Alongside interior projects for homes and public spaces, she designs and produces her own line of furniture and lighting, made together with skilled artisans. Her Raku-Yaki cabinet-bar was shown at the Grand Palais during the Révélations biennale.

The collections, each named as a series such as Isla, Ary or Oyo, include sofas, tables, sideboards, wall lights and beds. On Formground it is shown through 109 pieces, each linked to its own page.
