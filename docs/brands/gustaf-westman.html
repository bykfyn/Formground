Gustaf Westman is a Stockholm design studio founded in 2020 by Gustaf Westman, who trained as an architect. It makes made-to-order furniture, ceramics and glassware in bold colours, from the Chunky tables, stools and tableware to the Curvy mirrors and sideboards, the Blob sofa and the Pipe chairs.

Furniture is made by hand by woodworkers in Sweden, ceramics in Portugal and glassware is hand-blown in the Czech Republic. Most pieces come in many colours, so here each model is shown once, in its calmest colourway, with the others listed. On Formground it is shown through 62 pieces, each linked to its own page.
