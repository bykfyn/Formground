Articolo is a lighting studio in Richmond, Melbourne, with a showroom in New York, led by founder and creative director Nicci Kavals. Kavals worked as a chef and food stylist and as Melbourne editor of Vogue Entertaining & Travel before moving into sculptural lighting.

All lighting is handcrafted in Australia and made to order, by artisans from Murano glassblowers to Australian metalworkers. Indi, Fini, Occhi and Saddle are among the pieces shown here. On Formground it is shown through 102 pieces, each linked to its own page.
