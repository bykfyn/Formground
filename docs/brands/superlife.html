Superlife is a furniture and object brand in Yverdon-les-Bains, Switzerland, founded in 2014 by two designers from ECAL, with Edrris Gaaloul as creative director. It makes shelves, tables, lights, furniture and accessories, and began in a creative space made from his father's former forge.

Pieces are designed and made in Switzerland and produced in series. They are sold through the brand's online shop and through retailers. On Formground it is shown through 24 pieces, each linked to its own page.
