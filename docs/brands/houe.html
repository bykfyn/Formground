Houe is a Danish design house founded in 2007, with its address in Ry, Denmark, and a US address in California. The range centres on outdoor furniture, with indoor versions of some chairs: chairs, tables, lounge seating, lighting and a rug, designed together with designers including Henrik Pedersen, Thomas Pedersen and Roee Magdassi.

The furniture is designed to be repaired, and spare parts are offered for all collections. On Formground it is shown through 21 product families, each linked to its own page.
