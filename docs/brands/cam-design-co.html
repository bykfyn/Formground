CAM Design Co. is the furniture studio and workshop of Christopher Miano in the New York City area, with a wood and metal shop equipped for design, prototyping and fabrication. Its Squiggle series is shaped from solid American black walnut and other woods, in chairs, tables, consoles, a bench and bed, mirrors and lights.

Other lines include the Macaroni lights, with hand-turned red oak globes and a stated lead time of three to four weeks, the Marble Jointed stools, consoles and bench, and charred and ebonized pieces. On Formground it is shown through 31 pieces, each linked to its own page.
