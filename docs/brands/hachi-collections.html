Hachi Collections is a product and furniture design studio in Brooklyn, New York. Its range covers dining chairs and tables, benches, cabinets and credenzas, ceiling and wall lights and a round mirror, plus small wooden objects such as plates, a trivet and an aroma diffuser, in solid woods like white oak, walnut and maple, often with brass details.

The name is the Japanese reading of the number eight, which the studio ties to enriching people's lives. Furniture is made to order, with lead times and custom sizes and finishes listed on the product pages, and the NAMI series pairs sliced wood grain with epoxy. On Formground it is shown through 24 pieces, each linked to its own page.
