Frandsen is a Danish lighting company based in Horsens, Denmark. The collection is organised as pendant, table, ceiling, wall, floor, portable and outdoor lamps. Named series include the Ball pendants and wall lamps, the Lyss lamp series, Grasp, Flake, Nova and Hito.

Benny Frandsen is the company's founder, and the lamps are designed by a mix of designers, among them Toni Rie, Jonas Wagell, Friis & Moltke and Ida Tvede, together with Frandsen Design Studio. On Formground it is shown through 49 pieces, each linked to its own page.
