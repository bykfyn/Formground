Gärsnäs (Österlen, Skåne) · Founded 1893

Visit site →

Gärsnäs is a family-owned furniture maker in Gärsnäs, in Österlen in Skåne, Sweden. The carpenter Anders Andersson started the factory in 1893, and it is now run by the third generation of the Axelsson-Klockby family. The factory lies among red beech trees, and its chairs blend woodworking skill with industrial production.

The range is mainly chairs, armchairs, stools and tables, with 82 pieces shown on Formground, each linked to its own page. The modular construction of the chairs makes them easy to renovate.
