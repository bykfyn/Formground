Brdr. Krüger is a family-owned Danish furniture company, founded in Copenhagen in 1886 as a wood-turning workshop by the brothers Theodor and Ferdinand Krüger. It is now run by the fifth generation, siblings Julie and Jonas Krüger.

The furniture is made by hand at the company's own factory in Værløse and built to order, with a showroom at Bredgade 28 in Copenhagen. The collection includes designs by Hans Bølling and Nanna Ditzel. On Formground it is shown through 30 pieces, each linked to its own page.
