Larose Guyon

Verchères, Quebec · Founded 2015 · Made to order

Visit site →

Larose Guyon is a lighting and object design studio in Verchères, Quebec, Canada, founded in 2015 by Audrée L. Larose and Félix Guyon. It makes chandeliers and pendants in brass, copper, bronze or nickel, many with hand-blown glass globes, in named collections such as Rosette, Abysse, Saule and Perle.

Fixtures are assembled by hand in the Verchères workshop with the help of local artisans. Orders are made to order against a 50% deposit, with production taking 6 to 20 weeks depending on the model. On Formground it is shown through 30 pieces, each linked to its own page.
