Pat Kim is a designer and artist working from a waterfront studio in Red Hook, Brooklyn, making furniture, lamps and objects in wood, stone, cork and glass. The work moves between pure form exploration and tightly resolved functional pieces, from the stacked Cairn stools and the Cleave side table to the Phases coffee table, a glass top resting on turned wooden forms.

Lamps such as Double Bubble and Treble Bubble pair turned Douglas fir with glass globes, while kinetic Oloids, tabletop stabiles and hand-turned nutcrackers are sold through the studio's shop. Some pieces are one of a kind and others are made to order. On Formground it is shown through 20 pieces, each linked to its own page.
