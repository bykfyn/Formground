Copenhagen · Founded 2010

Visit site →

&Tradition is an international design brand founded in Copenhagen in 2010, built on a Danish legacy of craftsmanship and design. It designs the classics of tomorrow, from newly designed pieces to reissues, with a collection of furniture, lighting and interior objects that reaches back to mid-century icons.

The range includes Verner Panton's Flowerpot lamp, first produced in the late 1960s, and Arne Jacobsen and Flemming Lassen's Mayor sofa. On Formground it is shown through 406 pieces, each linked to its own page.
