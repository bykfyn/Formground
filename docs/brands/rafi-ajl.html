Rafi Ajl Studio is a furniture, lighting and glass studio in Berkeley, California, working in wood, glass and metal. Its tables pair hand-blown glass bases from the Saguaro process with wood or metal tops, alongside wood casegoods such as the Erratics and Grid series, mirrors, lighting and hand-made glassware.

Every piece is made to order in the studio and can be adjusted in size, material and finish, with lead times the studio puts at 12 to 16 weeks. The studio plants trees with the National Forest Foundation for each piece of furniture sold. On Formground it is shown through 33 pieces, each linked to its own page.
