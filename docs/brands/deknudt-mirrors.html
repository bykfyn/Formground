Deknudt Mirrors is a Belgian mirror maker in Deerlijk, run by the third generation of the Deknudt family since it began in 1946. Alongside made-to-measure mirror doors for bathroom cabinets, it designs and produces decorative mirrors, created by in-house designers and in collaboration with international designers such as Karim Rashid, Sylvain Willenz and Paul Cocksedge.

The decorative range is grouped into Signature, Essentials and Collectible lines and covers round, rectangular and irregular wall mirrors, with a few mirror-topped consoles and side tables. On Formground it is shown through 97 designs, each linked to its own page.
