Florian Gadsby is a ceramicist working in London, UK. He makes tableware, one-off decorative and sculptural vessels, and the occasional commission, throwing and trimming the pots himself. He trained as an apprentice with Lisa Hammond in London and spent six months in Mashiko, Japan, with Ken Matsuzaki. His book By My Hands: A Potter's Apprenticeship was published in 2023.

Work is sold in occasional online releases announced through his newsletter. On Formground it is shown through 94 pieces from these releases, mainly bowls, vases, jars and cups, each linked to its own page.
