Feldspar is a studio in rural Devon, England, run by Cath and Jeremy, who moved from London to Dartmoor in 2015 and began by making their own mug. It makes tableware, drinkware, vases and lighting in fine bone china, slip-cast and hand-painted in its own workshop, and has recently added a furniture collection by Jeremy in storm-felled local timber.

Feldspar also takes trade and bespoke commissions from interior designers, hotels and restaurants, and its work is sold through retailers including The Conran Shop, Selfridges, Fortnum & Mason and Harrods. On Formground it is shown through 177 pieces, each linked to its own page.
