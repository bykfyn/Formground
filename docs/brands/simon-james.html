Simon James is a design company in Auckland, founded in 2001, with a furniture and lighting showroom in Mt Eden and a homeware store in Herne Bay. It designs its own furniture and lighting and shows it alongside work by other designers.

The own-brand range covers sofas, lounge and dining chairs, tables and shelving, with part of the furniture made in New Zealand and listed with its own lead times. The company also takes on custom furniture projects. On Formground the studio's own designs are shown through 63 pieces; the other brands it sells are not included.
