Rut Fjågesund

CeramicsObjectsNorway
Visit site →

Rut Fjågesund is a Norwegian woodturner working from a smallholding in Lunde, Telemark, where a former knife workshop has become a small turnery. She turns bowls and other tableware mainly in maple, ash, birch and elm, all sourced and processed in Telemark.

Every piece in the shop is turned on a lathe, and all decoration is carved by hand with wood-carving gouges, in a clean, modern form language drawn from Nordic design. The timber can take a year or two from felling to be ready. On Formground she is shown through 19 pieces, from bowls to candle holders, plates, a tray and kitchen utensils, each linked to its own page.
