Kerryn Levy is a ceramic artist and maker working from a studio in Strathalbyn, South Australia. Her sculptural and functional vessels are hand-built by pinching, coiling and slab-building, and carry the finger marks and traces of the making process on the surface.

The shop includes the Onishi vases and vessels, Lumena, Onishi and Swan candle holders, and one-off sculptural vessels, some finished with kintsugi details in gold, alongside the ELIO side table. Part of the range is sold as pre-orders. On Formground it is shown through 29 pieces, each linked to its own page.
