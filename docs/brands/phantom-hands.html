Phantom Hands

FurnitureLightingObjectsIndia

Bangalore · Founded 2014

Visit site →

Phantom Hands is a furniture maker in Bangalore, India, working in teak and cane alongside carpenters, wood polishers, cane weavers and upholsterers from craft communities across the country. It began in 2014 as a catalogue of vintage objects.

Today it makes original collections and re-editions of South Asian modernist designs, including work by Geoffrey Bawa and the Chandigarh designers, with a stated production lead time of 6 to 12 weeks. On Formground it is shown through 88 pieces, each linked to its own page.
