Oi Soi Oi

LightingCeramicsObjectsDenmark

Founded 2007

Visit site →

Oi Soi Oi is a Danish company founded in 2007 by Britt Goodall and Christina Thorbøll, which designs and produces its own range for the home. Everything is handmade in Vietnam, where Britt Goodall has lived for more than twenty years and built the team that handles design and production.

The name is Vietnamese for "wow". The company blends clean Scandinavian lines with colourful Asian influences, and works with small family-run workshops rather than a factory. On Formground it is shown through 309 pieces.
