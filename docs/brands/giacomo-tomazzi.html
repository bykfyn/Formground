Giácomo Tomazzi Studio is a design studio in São Paulo, Brazil, founded in 2017 by the designer Giácomo Tomazzi. It designs furniture, lighting and objects, including the Solar and Meraki collections and the Bo armchair.

Alongside an in-house line of authored pieces produced in limited series, the studio designs products for Brazilian brands including América Móveis, Breton and La Fonte. The Bo armchair also exists in a limited edition. On Formground it is shown through 35 pieces, each linked to its own page.
