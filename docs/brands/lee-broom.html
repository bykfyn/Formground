Lee Broom is a London design brand founded by Lee Broom in 2007. He trained in fashion, worked for Vivienne Westwood and studied at Central Saint Martins before turning to furniture, lighting and accessories, and has since created more than 100 pieces.

The lighting range includes Hail, Vesper, Crescent, Fulcrum, Requiem and Orion, alongside furniture and accessories. Pieces are designed, manufactured and retailed under the Lee Broom name, with showrooms at Rivington Street in London and in New York. On Formground it is shown through 93 pieces, each linked to its own page.
