Jeff Martin Joinery is a furniture and sculpture studio in Vancouver, Canada, led by Jeff Martin, a sculptor who also runs a furniture design studio. The range includes ceramic Sarcophagus tables and wall cabinets, Neolith tables and stools in stone, bronze and resin, and Excavated furniture cast in aluminium and pewter, plus blown-glass lighting and vessels.

Pieces are ordered from the studio's own product line, and the studio ships internationally and offers a trade discount to designers and architects. Its work has been shown at Design Miami, Collective Design and Fuorisalone. On Formground it is shown through 74 pieces, each linked to its own page.
