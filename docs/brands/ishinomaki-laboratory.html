Ishinomaki · Founded 2011

Visit site →

Ishinomaki Laboratory is a furniture workshop in Ishinomaki, Miyagi, Japan, begun after the 2011 earthquake and tsunami as a public workshop where local people could build simple furniture themselves. It became a company in 2014 and still makes plain wooden furniture from standard timber sizes for everyday use.

The range includes the AA stool, the Ishinomaki bench, the Kobo lounge series of sofas and tables, the Drop umbrella stand and Jonah Takagi's Stave planter. Many designs are also sold as self-assembly kits, which are not shown here. On Formground it appears through 29 ready-made pieces, each linked to its own page.
