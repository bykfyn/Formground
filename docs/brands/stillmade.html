Visit site →

Stillmade is a furniture maker in Brooklyn, New York, founded in 2013, with Paul Mignogna as creative director. It designs and builds contemporary furniture for entryways, living and dining rooms, bedrooms and offices, building each piece individually in its Brooklyn workshop.

Most of the collection comes from its in-house studio, alongside an ongoing series of collaborations with other designers and artists, whose pieces are also made in the same workshop. Through its Stillworks programme it takes on product development for others. All furniture is made to order. On Formground it is shown through 67 pieces, each linked to its own page.
