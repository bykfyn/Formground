Clair Catillaz makes ceramics under the name CLAM LAB in Catskill, New York. The shop lists vases and vessels, pitchers, bowls, mugs and small treasure and soap dishes, in finishes such as ash, soda and copper bloom, with soda-fired and wood-fired work among them.

Pieces are listed one at a time, many of them numbered, and the range stretches from small mugs to large hand-built vessels, with a section for special and sculptural work and a page for custom commissions. On Formground it is shown through 80 pieces, each linked to its own page.
