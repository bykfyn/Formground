Tiptoe is a French furniture design studio founded in 2015, with a showroom in Paris. It designs tables, wall shelves, chairs, sofas, lamps and outdoor furniture, including the UNIT storage system, which customers can configure online.

Production is in Europe with partners working in wood, metal and recycled plastic, including a family metalworking firm in Haute-Savoie. Tiptoe is a B Corp certified company and sells through its own shop and a network of resellers. On Formground it is shown through 128 pieces, each linked to its own page.
