Silcohaus was founded in Sydney in 2023 by Alan and Vivi as an editorial and curation platform around colour, material and light. It brings together work by other designers and studios and also makes pieces through its in-house Studio Silco, which began with the Noka lamp and includes the Arno lamps and the Luno side tables.

The selection includes cabinets, sideboards and framed objects by Rayon Belge, the Mo_&_A bamboo floor lamp by HAO, the Volet lamp by S30 and the Hourglass T001 by Gwladys Faye. A showroom in Newtown hosts exhibitions. On Formground it is shown through 25 pieces, each linked to its own page.
