Resident is a furniture and lighting company based in Auckland, New Zealand, owned and run by its founders. It works with a group of designers including Asca Studio, Tim Rundle, Jamie McLellan, Philippe Malouin and NOTE, and the range includes the Sacha Sofa, the Radio Chair and the Cadre Chair.

Production runs through a network of workshops: lighting is made by a team in Auckland, timber furniture by joiners in Lithuania, and upholstery in London, Los Angeles and Melbourne. On Formground it is shown through 62 pieces, each linked to its own page.
