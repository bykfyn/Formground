Benchmark Furniture is a furniture maker in Kintbury, Berkshire, England, founded by Sean Sutcliffe and Terence Conran, with workshops in Berkshire and Dorset. It makes tables, seating, storage, planters and beds, among them the OVO, Sage, Victoria, Gleda, Iklwa and Chico ranges, and takes bespoke commissions for commercial and residential projects.

Milling, cabinet making, veneering, upholstery and metalwork are done in the workshops, and an apprenticeship scheme has run for over 35 years. On Formground it is shown through 112 pieces, each linked to its own page.
