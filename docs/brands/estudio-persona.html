Los Angeles

Visit site →

Estudio Persona is a furniture and lighting studio in Los Angeles, run by the Uruguayan designers Emiliana Gonzalez and Jessie Young, who have worked together in the city since 2015. The studio designs tables, seating, lighting and objects, such as the Tajo coffee table and the Abrazo lounge chair.

Its pieces are produced with Los Angeles manufacturers, and material or production constraints are treated as a starting point for the design. The studio has also collaborated with Pierre Frey, Movimento and BAINA. On Formground it is shown through 26 of its own pieces, each linked to its own page.
