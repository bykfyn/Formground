Gabriel Scott is a lighting studio in Montréal, Canada, founded in 2012 by Scott Richler, who came to lighting from architecture and jewellery design. Its chandeliers, pendants, sconces and desk lamps are built from blown glass, glass beads, brass and steel, and each fixture is approached as large-scale jewellery.

The range is organised in series such as Luna, Harlow, Briolette and Welles, and Welles has also been reworked with designers including David Rockwell, Alessandro Munge and Sybille de Margerie. Pieces are customisable and handmade to order. On Formground it is shown through 68 pieces, each linked to its own page.
