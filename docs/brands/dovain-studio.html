DOVAIN Studio makes lighting, furniture and objects, and also designs interiors. It was founded by Tiago Lima and Sergio Prieto, and Prieto, a Spanish artist-designer who lives and works in the north of Portugal, is its creative director.

The shop includes the Lola, Space and Game lamps, the Axis and Balance tables in wood and marble, the Bob, Elefante and Moreto sofas, and the Nube, Cloud and Luna mirrors. On Formground it is shown through 49 pieces, each linked to its own page.
