Sasha Court is a ceramic artist in Halifax, Nova Scotia. She builds each vessel by hand in stoneware, leaving finger marks, asymmetries and warps visible, and pairs soft contours with matte, textured finishes. The store's vases are watertight and carry poetic titles such as Walk on water and Almost reckless, alongside a few plates.

Alongside the store she takes private commissions for single vessels, with palette and shape agreed in dialogue. On Formground the work is shown through 27 pieces, each linked to its own page.
