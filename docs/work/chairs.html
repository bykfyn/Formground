About chairs

A chair is judged by sitting in it, which is the one thing a listing cannot offer. Some are made for a dining table and an hour at a time; others for a corner and a whole afternoon. What a chair is for, how long you will sit and what it sits at decide most of what matters.

Makers in Denmark (711 pieces), Italy (221), France (192) and Sweden (179) lead the listing. 106 of the 135 makers are independent. 47 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Decide what it is for and how long you will sit: a dining chair and a lounge chair are different things.
  • Check the seat height against your table or desk; a common gap is about 25-30 cm to the underside.
  • Look at the frame and the joints, and whether covers and seat pads can be replaced.
