About armchairs and lounge chairs

An armchair or lounge chair is the seat people stay in: for reading, for talking, for an evening. Comfort depends on the shape of the seat and back more than on how soft it looks, and the same chair can suit one person and not another. They range from compact wooden chairs with a cushion to deep, low lounge chairs, swivel chairs and chairs with a matching footstool.

Makers in Denmark (292 pieces), France (144), Italy (129) and Sweden (115) lead the listing. 92 of the 118 makers are independent. 26 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Seat height is commonly around 40-45 cm; a deep, low seat is harder to get out of.
  • Check the depth of the seat against your legs, and whether the back supports your lower back or your head.
  • Ask what the frame is made of, how the cushions are filled and whether the cover can be replaced.
