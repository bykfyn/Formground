About dining chairs

A dining chair is sat in for a meal and often for the hour after it, so seat height, depth and back support matter as much as looks. They range from slim wood and cane chairs to upholstered and moulded designs, with and without arms, and many come in several finishes.

Makers in Denmark (189 pieces), France (60), the United Kingdom (53) and Portugal (50) lead the listing. 34 of the 48 makers are independent. 9 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.
