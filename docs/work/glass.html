About glassware

This page gathers glassware: drinking glasses, wine and water glasses, tumblers, carafes and jugs, made by hand or machine. They range from thin, mouth-blown glass to heavy pressed and coloured glass, and from single glasses to sets.

Makers in Belgium (141 pieces), Denmark (54), Italy (46) and Sweden (22) lead the listing. 9 of the 19 makers are independent. 13 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.
