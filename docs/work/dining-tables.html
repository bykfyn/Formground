About dining tables

A dining table is the piece a room is arranged around, so its size, shape and base matter as much as its look. Tops are round, oval, rectangular or square; bases are four legs, trestles, a single pedestal or a sled; and many extend with leaves to seat more people.

Makers in Denmark (138 pieces), Sweden (92), Italy (89) and France (80) lead the listing. 62 of the 84 makers are independent. 14 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • A common rule of thumb is about 60 cm of table edge per person, 70 cm to sit comfortably.
  • Leave about 90-100 cm between the table edge and walls so chairs can be pulled out.
  • Solid wood can usually be refinished; stone and ceramic are heavy and can chip at the edges.
