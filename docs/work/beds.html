About beds

A bed frame is the biggest piece in a bedroom, and the mattress and base inside it matter as much as the look. Standards for size differ by country, so measuring both room and mattress is the first step. Frames range from simple platforms and slatted beds to upholstered beds, four-posters, daybeds and beds with storage.

Makers in Italy (47 pieces), the United Kingdom (23), Portugal (15) and Netherlands (13) lead the listing. 18 of the 24 makers are independent. 3 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Mattress sizes differ by country (for example 160 x 200 cm is common in Sweden); check the frame fits your mattress.
  • Check how the base supports the mattress: slats, a solid platform or a sprung base.
  • Measure the room, the door and the stairwell, as a bed has to get into the room.
