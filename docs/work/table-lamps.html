About table lamps

A table lamp lights a corner, a bedside or a desk, and it also furnishes the surface it sits on. The base, the shade and the height decide where the light falls and how the lamp looks in the room. Some give a soft glow across a room; others throw a tight beam for reading.

Makers in the United Kingdom (247 pieces), Denmark (139), France (132) and Belgium (100) lead the listing. 79 of the 102 makers are independent. 42 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Check where the shade's lower edge falls against your eyes when you are seated or in bed, to avoid glare.
  • Check the bulb fitting, the maximum wattage and whether it can be dimmed.
  • Look at where the switch is and how long the cable is before you buy.
