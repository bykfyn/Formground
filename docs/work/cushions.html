About cushions

Cushions add comfort and colour to a sofa, bed or chair. They range from wool, linen, cotton and velvet to knitted, quilted and printed designs, and from square and rectangular to round and bolster shapes. Some are sold as a cover only, without the inner.

Makers in Denmark (142 pieces), Sweden (119), Italy (73) and France (45) lead the listing. 19 of the 29 makers are independent. 12 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.
