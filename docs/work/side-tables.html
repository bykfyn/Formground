About side tables

A side table is the small surface beside a sofa, an armchair or a bed: somewhere to rest a drink, a lamp or a book without getting up. It is often the smallest piece in the room and the one that is moved the most. They range from tall slim tables and round pedestals to trays on legs, drum shapes and tables that slide over a chair arm.

Makers in France (182 pieces), Denmark (131), the United Kingdom (98) and Belgium (74) lead the listing. 92 of the 111 makers are independent. 8 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Match the height to the arm of the chair or sofa, commonly about the same or a little lower.
  • Check the top is big enough for what you will put on it, and whether it is stable if bumped.
  • A lamp on a side table needs a base that will not tip, and room for the cable.
