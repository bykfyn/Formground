About vases

A vase holds flowers, branches or dried stems, and is often an object worth looking at without them. They range from small bud vases to tall floor vases, in ceramic, glass, metal and stone, and from smooth thrown shapes to hand-built and sculptural forms.

Makers in Belgium (237 pieces), Denmark (200), France (198) and Italy (108) lead the listing. 38 of the 53 makers are independent. 61 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.
