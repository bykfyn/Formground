About sideboards

A sideboard is long, low storage that doubles as a surface: for a lamp, a vase, a record player or the serving dishes at dinner. It often anchors a wall, so proportion and the space in front of it matter. They range from slim sideboards on legs to wide, low units with sliding doors, drawers or both, in wood, lacquer, metal and stone.

Makers in France (53 pieces), Italy (50), the United Kingdom (30) and Sweden (20) lead the listing. 27 of the 37 makers are independent. 1 piece is new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Measure the wall and the room in front of it, including how far doors and drawers open.
  • Height is commonly about 70-90 cm; a lower unit suits a low sofa or a window.
  • Check the load rating, the leg or plinth, and whether it can be fixed to the wall.
