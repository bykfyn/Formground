About pendant lamps

A pendant lamp hangs from the ceiling on a cable, chain or rod, and it does two jobs at once: it lights a place and it marks that place out in the room. Over a dining table, a kitchen island or a bedside it works as a focal point; over a stairwell or hallway it works as a landmark. Pendants range from a single shade on a long drop to clusters, rows and linear fittings, and from bare bulbs to deep opaque domes.

Makers in Denmark (234 pieces), the United Kingdom (152), the United States (127) and France (119) lead the listing. 38 of the 49 makers are independent. 13 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Over a table, the lower edge is commonly hung about 75-85 cm above the surface.
  • Check the fitting, the maximum wattage and whether it can be dimmed.
  • Fixed wiring should be done by a qualified electrician; rules differ by country.
