About rugs

A rug does more than cover a floor: it warms a room, softens the sound in it and ties a group of furniture together. The size and the position matter more than the pattern. They range from hand-knotted and hand-woven rugs to tufted, flat-woven and machine-made ones, in wool, cotton, jute, silk, synthetic fibres and blends.

Makers in Sweden (216 pieces), Denmark (194), Spain (109) and France (84) lead the listing. 24 of the 40 makers are independent. 32 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Size matters most: in a living room, the front legs of the sofa and chairs commonly sit on the rug.
  • Pile height and fibre decide how it feels underfoot and how it wears and cleans.
  • Ask how the rug was made and by whom; some certifications cover this, and each covers one aspect only.
