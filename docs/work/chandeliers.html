About chandeliers

A chandelier hangs several lights together from one point on the ceiling, as arms, rings, tiers or clusters of glass, brass or other materials. It lights a room and is meant to be looked at: over a dining table, in an entrance or stairwell, or in a living room with a high ceiling. Modern chandeliers run from classical crystal to open, sculptural frames.

Makers in Italy (250 pieces), the United States (65), the United Kingdom (29) and Denmark (23) lead the listing. 24 of the 29 makers are independent. 7 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Check the weight and the ceiling fixing before anything else.
  • Count the bulbs: the total wattage and how often you will have to change them.
  • Plan how you will reach it for cleaning and changing bulbs.
