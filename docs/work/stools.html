About stools

A stool is the most flexible seat in a home: at a table, beside a bed, in a hallway to put on shoes, at a worktop or as an extra surface. With no back it takes little space and is easy to move. They range from low three-legged stools and stacking stools to padded seats, step stools and tall stools for counters.

Makers in Denmark (401 pieces), France (98), Italy (84) and the United Kingdom (70) lead the listing. 108 of the 130 makers are independent. 137 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Match the height to the surface: commonly around 45 cm at a dining table, 60-65 cm at a counter.
  • Check the seat shape and size, because with no back the seat does all the work.
  • Three legs sit steadier on an uneven floor; four need a level one.
