About shelving

Shelving holds what you own, so the weight it takes and how it is fixed matter before the look. Open shelves show everything; deeper or taller ones give more room but need more care. They range from wall-mounted shelves and rails to freestanding bookcases, ladder shelves and modular systems that grow.

Makers in Denmark (84 pieces), France (39), Italy (34) and Sweden (26) lead the listing. 35 of the 49 makers are independent. 5 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Check the load per shelf: books are heavy, commonly around 20-25 kg per metre of shelf.
  • Tall or freestanding shelving should be fixed to the wall to stop it tipping.
  • Wall-mounted shelves need fixings that suit your wall (concrete, brick, studs or plasterboard).
