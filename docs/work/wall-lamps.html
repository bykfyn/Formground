About wall lamps

A wall lamp, also called a wall light or sconce, fixes to the wall and frees the floor and the table. It can wash a wall with light, read beside a bed, mark a hallway or flank a mirror. Some are wired into the wall, some plug in and carry a visible cable, and some swing out on an arm.

Makers in Italy (108 pieces), Denmark (94), France (78) and Belgium (63) lead the listing. 48 of the 64 makers are independent. 9 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Decide the height from where you will sit or stand, then check the lamp against it.
  • Wired-in or plug-in changes the installation and what the wall looks like.
  • Check how far the lamp projects from the wall, especially in a hallway or by a door.
