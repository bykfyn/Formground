About ceiling lamps

A ceiling lamp sits on the ceiling or hangs only a short way below it, as a flush or semi-flush fitting. It gives the general light of a room, and where there is no space or height for a pendant it is often the main source. Shades range from a plain disc to sculptural domes, clusters and linear fittings.

Makers in Italy (82 pieces), the United States (74), France (40) and Denmark (30) lead the listing. 27 of the 38 makers are independent. 3 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • A lower ceiling usually suits a flush or semi-flush fitting.
  • Check the size against the room, and how the light spreads, not only how the lamp looks.
  • Check how the bulb or LED is replaced once the lamp is up.
