About benches

A bench seats two or more people along one length. It works at a dining table in place of chairs, at the end of a bed, in a hallway for shoes and coats, or against a wall as extra seating. They range from solid wood and stone benches to upholstered and outdoor designs, some with storage.

Makers in Belgium (78 pieces), Denmark (65), Netherlands (51) and France (36) lead the listing. 72 of the 90 makers are independent. 3 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.
