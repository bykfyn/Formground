About bar stools

A bar stool lifts you to counter or bar height, so the choice starts with a measurement. A stool that is too tall or too low is uncomfortable however good it looks. They range from backless stools and stools with a low back to full chairs on tall legs, in fixed, swivel and adjustable-height versions.

Makers in Denmark (300 pieces), Sweden (30), the United Kingdom (26) and Portugal (23) lead the listing. 49 of the 66 makers are independent. 115 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Measure the counter, not the room: counter height is commonly 85-95 cm, bar height 105-115 cm.
  • Leave about 25-30 cm between the seat and the underside of the surface.
  • Look for a footrest, a stable base and floor protection.
