About bedside tables

A bedside table holds what you reach for at night: a lamp, a book, a glass of water, a phone. They are made to sit close to the height of the mattress. They range from open tables and trays on legs to cabinets with drawers or doors, and wall-hung designs that leave the floor clear.

Makers in France (22 pieces), Netherlands (18), the United Kingdom (16) and Italy (12) lead the listing. 18 of the 25 makers are independent. 3 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.
