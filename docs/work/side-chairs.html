About side chairs

A side chair is a lighter chair that is not tied to one table. It can stand against a wall in a hallway, sit at a desk or add a seat in a living room, and many are also used at dining tables. They range from slim wood and metal frames to upholstered seats, with and without arms.

Makers in Denmark (23 pieces), the United Kingdom (12), France (8) and Italy (5) lead the listing. 7 of the 13 makers are independent. 6 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.
