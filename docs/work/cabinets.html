About cabinets

A cabinet is storage that stays in view, so how it opens, how it stands and what it is made of matter as much as what it holds. They range from low units and tall display cabinets to glazed cases, cupboards on legs and wall-mounted cabinets, with doors, drawers or both.

Makers in Netherlands (170 pieces), Denmark (40), France (25) and Sweden (22) lead the listing. 24 of the 36 makers are independent. 7 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Measure the space and the clear room needed for doors and drawers to open.
  • Tall or top-heavy cabinets should be fixed to the wall to prevent tipping.
  • Check the hinges, runners and shelves, and whether the shelves are adjustable.
