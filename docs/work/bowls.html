About bowls

Bowls work on the table and on the shelf: serving and salad bowls, soup and cereal bowls, fruit bowls, and bowls that are simply objects. They range from thrown ceramic and glass to wood, metal and stone, in sizes from a small dish to a wide centrepiece.

Makers in Belgium (321 pieces), Denmark (91), Italy (75) and the United Kingdom (74) lead the listing. 28 of the 41 makers are independent. 21 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.
