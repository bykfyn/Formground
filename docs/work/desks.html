About desks

A desk is a work surface you use for hours, so its height, depth and edges matter more than its looks. Where it stands, the light it gets and the cables it has to hide are part of the choice. They range from slim writing desks and console desks to large work desks, standing and height-adjustable desks and desks with drawers.

Makers in France (54 pieces), Italy (36), the United Kingdom (36) and Denmark (27) lead the listing. 40 of the 54 makers are independent. 8 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Desk height is commonly about 72-75 cm; check your chair, elbows and screen.
  • Allow depth for a screen: around 60-80 cm is common.
  • Check where cables, sockets and light will go before you choose.
