About ottomans and poufs

Ottomans and poufs are low, soft seats without a back. They are used as extra seating, as a footrest or, with a tray on top, as a small table. They range from round cushioned poufs in wool, leather and knit to large rectangular ottomans, some with a hinged lid and storage inside.

Makers in Denmark (81 pieces), Italy (43), Belgium (24) and Portugal (18) lead the listing. 30 of the 51 makers are independent.
