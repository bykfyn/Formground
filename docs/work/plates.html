About plates

Plates range from dinner and side plates to platters, serving plates and decorative wall plates. They are made in porcelain, stoneware, earthenware and glass, plain or patterned, and are often sold singly as well as in sets.

Makers in Belgium (600 pieces), Italy (80), Denmark (31) and the United Kingdom (22) lead the listing. 14 of the 26 makers are independent. 31 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.
