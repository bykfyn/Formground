About coffee tables

A coffee table sits between the people and the things they put down: a cup, a book, a candle, a remote. It anchors a seating group and decides how easily you can move through it. They range from low round tables to long rectangular ones, nests of two or three, tables with a shelf and tables that lift to become a work surface.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Height is commonly about the same as the sofa seat, or slightly lower, around 35-45 cm.
  • Leave about 40-50 cm between the table and the sofa so you can reach it and still walk by.
  • Check how the top handles heat, water and scratches, and what the edges are like at knee height.
