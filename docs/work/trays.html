About trays

A tray carries, serves and gathers: breakfast and drinks, small things on a table, or keys and post by the door. They range from small catch-all trays to large serving trays, in wood, metal, ceramic, leather and woven materials.

Makers in Denmark (123 pieces), Sweden (68), Belgium (45) and Italy (38) lead the listing. 32 of the 44 makers are independent. 31 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.
