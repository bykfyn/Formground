About console tables

A console table is a narrow table made to stand against a wall: in a hallway, behind a sofa or under a mirror. It holds keys, a lamp, a bowl or books without taking much floor space. They range from slim wood and metal designs to marble-topped and sculptural ones, with and without drawers.

Makers in France (98 pieces), the United Kingdom (50), Sweden (30) and Italy (19) lead the listing. 41 of the 52 makers are independent. 4 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.
