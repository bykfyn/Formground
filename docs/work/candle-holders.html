About candle holders

A candle holder gives a flame somewhere safe and steady to stand, and it is often the part of the arrangement that stays on the table long after the candle has gone. They range from single tapers and pillar holders to tealight cups, candelabras and wall-mounted sconces, in glass, ceramic, metal, wood and stone.

Makers in Denmark (89 pieces), Sweden (48), the United States (40) and the United Kingdom (29) lead the listing. 75 of the 94 makers are independent. 21 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Check the holder suits the candle: taper, pillar and tealight holders are different things, and taper sizes vary.
  • Check the base is wide or weighted enough to stay upright; taller holders tip more easily.
  • Use on a level, heat-resistant surface, away from anything that burns, and never leave a candle burning unattended.
