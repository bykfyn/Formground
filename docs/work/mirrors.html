About mirrors

A mirror does a practical job, helping you see yourself, and a spatial one, because it bounces light and doubles a view. Where it hangs and what it reflects matter as much as the frame. They range from full-length floor mirrors and tall leaning mirrors to wall mirrors in round, oval and arched shapes, mirrors with shelves or lights, and small tabletop mirrors.

Makers in France (146 pieces), Italy (73), Denmark (72) and the United Kingdom (66) lead the listing. 54 of the 78 makers are independent. 11 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Hang it so the centre sits at about eye level, commonly around 145-155 cm from the floor.
  • Think about what it will reflect: a window brings light in; clutter looks twice as big.
  • A heavy mirror needs fixings rated for its weight and the wall, and often two people to hang it.
