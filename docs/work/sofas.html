About sofas

A sofa is the largest piece of seating in most homes and the one you live with longest, so comfort, size and fabric matter more than a photo suggests. They range from compact two-seaters to modular systems that grow and rearrange, and from firm, upright frames to deep lounging seats.

Makers in Denmark (305 pieces), France (164), the United Kingdom (124) and Italy (108) lead the listing. 65 of the 88 makers are independent. 17 pieces are new from their makers in the last 90 days.

Buying guide: what to consider

  • Measure the route in (doors, stairwells, lifts) as well as the room.
  • "Two-seater" differs between makers, so compare the width in centimetres.
  • A higher fabric abrasion rating (often a Martindale number) means more resistance to wear.
