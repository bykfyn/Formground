## Par en Par  (79 pieces on Formground)

Par en Par is an Argentine design brand that makes furniture and home objects combining art and design, from stools and tables to consoles, armchairs, beds, lamps and rugs. Materials named on the site include recycled plastic, lacquered and reclaimed wood, and folded sheet steel with epoxy paint.

The work is built on collaboration with a network of craftspeople, designers and creatives, and pieces are produced as limited editions in small quantities. Many items are made to order, with delivery times of 60 to 90 days. On Formground it is shown through 79 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Stackelbergs  (77 pieces on Formground)

Stackelbergs is a family-owned Swedish maker of mohair and wool throws, blankets and accessories, founded by Anna-Carin Stackelberg and active for more than 15 years. Its range includes a collection with Carl and Karin Larsson, inspired by their home.

The throws are designed in Sweden and produced by partner producers in Italy, South Africa and Ukraine using traditional craft methods, with handmade fringes. Customised products are offered for projects with special design requirements, and the range is sold through shops across Europe. On Formground it is shown through 77 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Joris Poggioli  (74 pieces on Formground)

Joris Poggioli is an architect and designer of French-Italian heritage who has worked on furniture, lighting and objects under his own name since 2017. The pieces are sculptural, with the emphasis on form and on materials such as black bronze, marble, onyx and rosewood. The 2025 series of numbered models includes a bench, chair, stool and desk.

Many pieces are listed as limited editions of 12, and some are numbered and signed. Lead times are given as 12 to 14 weeks. Alongside the design work, the practice also takes on architecture projects. On Formground it is shown through 74 pieces, each linked to its own page.

Facts: Type: Designer | Products: Furniture, lighting and objects | Founded: 2017 | Website: jorispoggioli.com

Sources: Joris Poggioli - Design & Architecture (https://jorispoggioli.com/)

## Parachilna  (73 pieces on Formground)

Parachilna is a lighting brand founded in 2013 and based in Colonia Güell, Santa Coloma de Cervelló, near Barcelona, Spain. It builds on the Riera family's lighting history, which began in the 1930s with Metalarte. The range covers pendant, floor, wall and ceiling, table and outdoor lights in collections such as Aballs, Babel, Gweilo, Petra and Topuro.

Parachilna works with a circle of designers including Jaime Hayon, Sebastian Herkner, Stephen Burks, Neri&Hu and Atelier Oï. Components come from European suppliers and production relies on highly manual methods, with lights designed to be demountable and repairable. On Formground it is shown through 73 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Master & Master  (72 pieces on Formground)
