## Oblure  (34 pieces on Formground)

Oblure is a Swedish lighting brand based in Gothenburg, founded in 2017 by Markus Johansson and Erik Möller. It designs pendant, wall, table and floor lamps for homes and public spaces, and its first lamp, Cirrata, began as Johansson's thesis project in 2011.

Oblure develops its lamps together with international designers, among them Victor Castanera (Balance) and Färg & Blanch (Lightbone). Production is spread across several countries, with plans to grow manufacturing in Sweden. On Formground it is shown through 34 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Massimo Copenhagen  (34 pieces on Formground)

Massimo Copenhagen is a Danish rug company based in Copenhagen with a showroom in Birkerød. It sells handmade contemporary rugs for private homes and public spaces, woven in India by weavers whose skills are passed down through generations, in materials such as bamboo, hemp, jute and wool.

The range runs to more than 350 variations, most of them held in stock, and the company also offers custom rugs for private and contract projects. Designer collaborations include Norm Architects and Space Copenhagen. On Formground it is shown through 34 pieces, each linked to its own page.

Facts: Country: Denmark | City: Copenhagen | Type: Maker | Products: Rugs | Website: massimo.dk

Sources: Our Story (https://massimo.dk/pages/our-story); Massimo Copenhagen (https://massimo.dk)

## Kin and Co  (34 pieces on Formground)

Kin & Company is a design practice in Brooklyn, New York, run by cousins Joseph Vidich, an architect, and Kira de Paola, an interior designer. It makes sculptural metal furniture and objects, among them the Thin Series and the Crescent Table, and its first collection was shown in 2017.

The studio works with steel and tests the limits of the material, including research into oxidation and patinas, where chemical catalysts speed up the metal's natural oxidation to create surface patterns. On Formground it is shown through 34 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Arturel  (34 pieces on Formground)

Arturel is a Danish acoustics and interiors company with its headquarters in Aabyhøj, Aarhus, and an office in Brooklyn, New York. It makes sound-absorbing wall art, acoustic tiles and rugs, including a series with upcycled wool textiles from Kvadrat framed in handmade solid oak.

## Anour  (34 pieces on Formground)

Anour is a Danish lighting maker based in Copenhagen, producing handcrafted contemporary lamps in metals such as brass, copper, stainless steel and aluminium. The range covers ceiling, pendant, wall, table and floor lamps, and the I-MODEL, designed by founder Arash Nourinejad, is presented as its signature lamp.

Production takes place in Denmark, where craftspeople cut, shape, sand, oil and brush each piece by hand and assemble every lamp by hand; a single lamp takes several weeks to produce. The brand also supplies professionals and trade customers. On Formground it is shown through 34 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Kalmar Werkstätten  (33 pieces on Formground)

Kalmar Werkstätten is the product line of J.T. Kalmar, a family-run lighting maker founded in Vienna in 1881 by Julius August Kalmar and now in its fifth generation. Launched in 2009, the line makes pendant, table and floor luminaires based on designs from the company's archives, including the Posthorn pendant and the Kilo table lamp.

The archive pieces draw on the company's work with architects of the Austrian Werkbund and are updated for current interiors. The same company also runs Kalmar Project, which makes custom lighting for hospitality, commercial and marine clients. On Formground it is shown through 33 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Pulkra  (28 pieces on Formground)

Pulkra is a concrete design company in the Veneto region of Italy, with its office in Silea and its production site in Susegana. It makes furniture and objects such as the Multiplo Chair and the Dome Bathtub in ACRON, a composite concrete containing polymers and fine marble sand.

Pulkra works with designers and emerging artists, including Stormo Studio and Finemateria, and produces its pieces in limited editions. Under its Time Loop programme it takes back products at the end of their life so the material can be recycled. On Formground it is shown through 28 pieces, each linked to its own page.
