## Resident  (24 pieces on Formground)

Resident is a furniture and lighting company, owned and run by its founder, with its headquarters in Albany, Auckland, New Zealand. It works with designers from around the world, among them Philippe Malouin, Tim Rundle and Michael Young, on seating, tables, pendants and table lamps.

Production is spread across a network of workshops. A team in Auckland makes the lighting in-house, timber joinery is done in Lithuania, and upholstery is handled by small workshops in London, Los Angeles and Melbourne. Pieces are sold directly and through stockists. On Formground it is shown through 24 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Esther Knopfler  (24 pieces on Formground)

## Baleri Italia  (24 pieces on Formground)

Baleri Italia is a furniture company based in Cantù, Italy, founded on 8 March 1984 by Enrico Baleri and Marilisa Decimo. It makes chairs, armchairs, sofas, tables, modular units and small objects, and its first major collaborator was the designer Philippe Starck.

The range includes reissues such as Alessandro Mendini's Amiko coat hanger from 1988, alongside newer work by designers including Odo Fioravanti and Claesson Koivisto Rune. The company treats designing pieces for assembly as a core part of its approach. On Formground it is shown through 24 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Editions Midi  (23 pieces on Formground)

Éditions Midi is a furniture and object brand based in Provence, France, begun in 2019 by interior architect Jérémie du Chaffaut. It makes contemporary pieces inspired by regional craft, such as the Radassié oak banquette and chairs with seats of sagne, a rush from the Rhône banks.

The pieces are produced with a small group of artisans, including a chair-maker in the Camargue and ceramicists near Aix-en-Provence and in Marseille, and each chair is assembled by hand with pegged joints. A separate studio service takes custom commissions. On Formground it is shown through 23 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Sekt  (20 pieces on Formground)

Sekt is a Swedish maker of lighting and interior objects, operating as Sekt Design AB with a showroom in Malmö that is open by appointment. Its pieces are made by small-scale manufacturers in Sweden and nearby, using methods such as mouth-blown glass, sand-cast bronze and forged metal.
