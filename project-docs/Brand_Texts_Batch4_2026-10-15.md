## Luke Malaney  (53 pieces on Formground)

Luke Malaney is a furniture maker who lives and works in Brooklyn, New York, and was born on Long Island. He trained under a classical Roman woodworker and applies traditional craft techniques in new ways, making work that sits between furniture and sculpture.

His pieces have been shown in solo, duo and group exhibitions since 2022, including a two-person show at Marc Straus Gallery in New York and, with Objective Gallery, at Design Miami and Thema Fair in Paris. Inquiries about commissions and collaborations go directly to him. On Formground it is shown through 53 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Buro Berger  (52 pieces on Formground)

## La Chambre d'Ami  (50 pieces on Formground)

La Chambre d'Ami is a French home decoration brand based in Rennes, Brittany. ## Källemo  (50 pieces on Formground)

Källemo is a Swedish furniture company based in Värnamo, founded in 1973 by Sven Lundh and run today by his daughter Karin Lundh. The collection covers chairs, armchairs and sofas, tables, shelves and cabinets, including the Ettore table by Åsa Jungnelius, which received the Born Classic prize.

Collections are aimed at architects and retailers who share a long-term view of quality, and the furniture is designed to outlast passing trends. The range includes a limited editions section and an archive of discontinued pieces. On Formground it is shown through 50 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Bocci  (48 pieces on Formground)

Bocci is a lighting maker headquartered in Vancouver, Canada, founded in 2005 under the creative direction of Omer Arbel. It makes glass and metal lighting and installations, and numbers each project in the order it was conceived, so its lamps are grouped in series such as 14, 28 and 141.

Every object is developed, engineered and fabricated in-house at the Vancouver factory, and each piece is handmade and therefore unique. Bocci also offers an online configurator for building an installation to suit a space. On Formground it is shown through 48 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Art Resources  (48 pieces on Formground)

Art Resources is a family-owned maker of furniture, lighting, mirrors and decorative objects in Bangkok, Thailand, founded in 1989. Its pieces draw on Art Deco, combine simple geometric forms with local Thai craftsmanship, and use materials such as antiqued mirror, shagreen, rattan and bronze.

Bespoke work is a core service: a dedicated team guides clients through the available materials and builds custom pieces to the same standard as the standard range. The company has worked with interior designers and architects on residences and hotels. On Formground it is shown through 48 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Eilersen  (38 pieces on Formground)

Eilersen is a furniture maker based in Skamby, Denmark, with a history that goes back to 1895. It makes sofas, chairs, tables, shelves and accessories, and offers fabrics and leather for its upholstery. Its sofas are built on a solid pinewood frame, with timber oven-dried to a moisture content of 8-10 percent and traceable to FSC-certified sources.

The range is split into the main Eilersen collection and separate Contract, Outdoor and Viable lines. Eilersen has showrooms in Skamby and Copenhagen and a dealer section for trade customers. On Formground it is shown through 38 pieces, each linked to its own page.
