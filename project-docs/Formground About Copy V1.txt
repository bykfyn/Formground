About — Formground
A place to discover work from independent makers.
Formground brings together independent makers of design led products with those who are looking for something original.
Why this exists
Small studios and independent makers create beautiful, original work, but they rarely have the marketing budget or presence to be found alongside established brands. Search for a specific kind of furniture, lighting, ceramics, or object, and you'll find established brands long before you find the work of independent makers. Not because the work isn't there — because visibility was never built for them.
Formground exists to close that gap.
A curated community
Formground searches through a curated community of makers, not an open directory. No one pays to be part of the community, no one pays to be found, no one pays to rank higher, and every result leads straight back to the makers themselves.
There are no filters, no categories to click through — simply describe what you're after, the way you'd describe it to a friend. Formground is for discovery — it simply helps you find something original and links you to the maker's site.
For makers
If you'd like to be part of the community and found on Formground, get in touch — and we'll take a look. If you're already here to be found we will let you know how it's going — no dashboard, no login, just a simple summary to your inbox.
Would you rather not be featured on Formground? Just let us know and we will remove you.
Where things stand
Formground is still early — a small, growing community of independent makers, built thoughtfully rather than quickly. It won't try to be everything — just useful, and honest about how it works.
