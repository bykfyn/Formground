## Miniforms  (117 pieces on Formground)

Miniforms is an Italian furniture company based in Meolo, north of Venice. It makes tables, seating, padded furniture, storage, coffee tables, lighting and accessories for homes, hospitality and offices. Luigi Bardini started the business in wooden furniture, and his sons took it over in 2009.

Production relies on craft trades including carpenters, glassblowers, ceramic shapers and upholsterers, with machines assisting the hand work. Since 2009 the company has chosen young designers and moved towards rounder, more colourful shapes. On Formground it is shown through 117 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Tolix  (85 pieces on Formground)

Tolix is a French furniture maker founded in 1927 in Autun, Burgundy. It produces steel chairs, stools, benches, tables, storage and accessories for indoor and outdoor use, and introduced galvanisation to French furniture manufacturing. The T37 chair was created for the 1937 Paris world exhibition and was reissued in 2023.

Each piece is built in series through manual steps: cutting, stamping, bending, welding, sanding and painting, with quality checked at every stage. Collections are made in Autun or by selected European partners, and the company keeps a showroom in Paris, open by appointment. On Formground it is shown through 85 pieces, each linked to its own page.
