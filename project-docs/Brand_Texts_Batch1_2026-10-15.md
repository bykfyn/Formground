## Galerie Kreo  (1,008 pieces on Formground)

Galerie kreo is a design gallery in Paris, founded in 1999 by Clémence and Didier Krzentowski. It produces contemporary furniture and objects with designers such as Konstantin Grcic, Jasper Morrison and Hella Jongerius, and it also shows French and Italian lighting from the 1950s to the 1980s. A second gallery opened in London's Mayfair in 2014.

The contemporary pieces are made in limited series, created by the designers exclusively for the gallery. The Paris gallery has been at 31 rue Dauphine in Saint-Germain-des-Prés since 2008. On Formground it is shown through 1,008 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## The Conran Shop  (707 pieces on Formground)

The Conran Shop is a design shop based in London, opened in 1973 on Fulham Road by Sir Terence Conran. It sells furniture, lighting, kitchen and dining items, rugs, cushions and gifts, including work by outside designers it collaborates with, such as the Pieces by Charlie Sheppard collection.

Alongside that range, the shop sells exclusive designs drawn in-house by its London team, TCS Studio, and made by manufacturers and artisans internationally. It lists the United Kingdom and the Middle East as its markets. On Formground it is shown through 707 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Eric Schmitt Studio  (385 pieces on Formground)

Eric Schmitt is a self-taught designer and craftsman whose design office and workshop sit on the edge of the Fontainebleau forest in France. He makes furniture, lighting, vases and objects, along with a jewellery line, working in hammered wrought iron, bronze and marble. His first pieces were hand-forged with a power hammer.

A design starts as a drawing that becomes a model, which founders, glassmakers and marble workers produce. The parts return to the atelier to be patinated and assembled. The work is also carried by galleries and editors including Galerie Dutko, Maison Liaigre and Carpenters Workshop Gallery. On Formground it is shown through 385 pieces, each linked to its own page.

Facts: Country: France | Type: Designer | Products: Furniture, lighting, vases and objects in metal and stone | Website: ericschmitt.com

Sources: Eric Schmitt, Informations (https://www.ericschmitt.com/informations_eric-schmitt); Eric Schmitt, L'Atelier (https://www.ericschmitt.com/informations_atelier); Eric Schmitt, home (https://www.ericschmitt.com/)

## Paradisverkstaden  (341 pieces on Formground)

Paradisverkstaden is a family-run ceramics workshop and shop in Färjestaden on Öland, Sweden, making handmade ceramics there since 1974. The range covers tableware, candle holders, vases and planters, with stoneware used for the Lantliv series and glazes in oat, brown and natural tones.

Pieces are thrown by hand in the workshop, and some are decorated with hand-painted stripes. The business also runs a café and an art hall, and has made a collection with Ovland. On Formground it is shown through 341 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## De La Espada  (317 pieces on Formground)

De La Espada is a furniture maker founded in 1993 by Fatima De La Espada and Luis De Oliveira, with its main workshop in Mira on Portugal's Silver Coast. It makes beds, tables, chairs, sofas, desks and storage in solid wood and upholstery, and uses Manta Lido, a hand-woven merino wool textile from the Alentejo region.

Furniture is built to order by craftspeople, designers and product engineers who share one workshop. Designs come from its in-house De La Espada Atelier and from outside designers such as Neri&Hu, Luca Nichetto and Sam Baron. It also runs a gallery in Porto. On Formground it is shown through 317 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Rihouse  (282 pieces on Formground)

Rihouse is a design space and online shop in Barcelona, Spain, linked to the studio of designer Francesc Rifé. It is a retailer: it sells furniture, lighting, objects and books, including pieces the studio designed for other brands, so it does not make all of them itself. It also offers the RI Collection, a furniture line that revives designs from the studio.

The physical space at Josep Bertrand 3 has two levels, an open showroom and a quieter lower floor. It works with artists, emerging designers and contemporary artisans, and the RI Collection includes new made-to-measure furniture. On Formground it is shown through 282 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Magis  (251 pieces on Formground)

Magis is an Italian furniture company founded in 1976 by Eugenio Perazza and based in Torre di Mosto, in the Veneto region. It makes chairs, stools, tables, sofas, storage, lighting and outdoor furniture. Its catalogue includes Konstantin Grcic's Chair_One, a die-cast aluminium chair from 2003, and Jasper Morrison's Air-Chair, a polypropylene chair made with air-moulding.

Magis works with outside designers, among them Jasper Morrison, Konstantin Grcic and Naoto Fukasawa, and chooses materials and production methods project by project. Logistics, assembly, offices and a showroom share one site in Torre di Mosto, in use since 2010. On Formground it is shown through 251 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Asplund  (221 pieces on Formground)

Asplund is a design retailer in Stockholm, Sweden, founded in 1990, with a store and showroom on Sibyllegatan and a webshop. ## Maruni  (164 pieces on Formground)

Maruni is a wood furniture maker in Hiroshima, Japan, founded in 1928 as Showa Bentwood Factory and renamed Maruni Wood Industry in 1933. It makes chairs, sofas, stools and tables, including the Hiroshima series developed under art director Naoto Fukasawa, which has a natural wood finish with almost no paint or varnish.

Maruni works with outside designers: Fukasawa has been art director since 2010, Jasper Morrison joined in 2011 and Cecilie Manz in 2022. The factory is in Hiroshima prefecture and showrooms are in Tokyo, Osaka, Hiroshima, Hatsukaichi and Fukuoka. On Formground it is shown through 164 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## GATOMIKIO  (136 pieces on Formground)

## B&B Italia  (129 pieces on Formground)

B&B Italia is an Italian furniture maker founded in 1966 as C&B, a joint venture between Cassina and Busnelli started by Piero Ambrogio Busnelli. It makes indoor and outdoor sofas, armchairs, tables, beds and storage, and brought cold-moulded polyurethane foam to upholstered furniture, as in Gaetano Pesce's UP series of 1969.

Designers working with the company include Antonio Citterio, Piero Lissoni, Patricia Urquiola and Vincent Van Duysen. Fabrics, leathers and finishes can be customised on request, and the furniture is sold through B&B Italia stores and authorised dealers. On Formground it is shown through 129 pieces, each linked to its own page.

## Catellani & Smith  (126 pieces on Formground)
